The best Koala AI alternatives in 2026 are Rankbox for a fixed 30 articles a month on autopilot, SEOWriting.ai and Machined for the lowest price per article, and Junia AI for scheduled publishing to many CMSs. Autoblogging.ai suits Amazon review and news sites, and Byword suits programmatic SEO with a team.

This guide is about Koala AI at koala.sh, the AI SEO writer behind KoalaWriter. It's not about Koala at getkoala.com, a B2B sales tool whose site says it was acquired by Cursor and shuts down on 30 September.

Koala sells words, not articles. Its $49 Professional plan includes 100,000 words a month and its $99 Boost plan 250,000, and the writing model you pick changes how far those words go. Scheduled autopilot publishing starts at Boost. So people compare Koala AI alternatives to know what a month of articles will cost, or to get autopilot for less than $99.

Every price below was checked on 23 September 2026, on each vendor's own pricing page. We make Rankbox, so it's listed first. We've been just as plain about its trade-offs, and about the cases where Koala is still the better buy. For SEO data, affiliate content, and languages, that's often.

## Key Takeaways

- Koala's Starter plan is $25 a month for 45,000 words, Professional $49 for 100,000, and Boost $99 for 250,000. Yearly billing takes 20% off.
- The model sets the article count. By our arithmetic, Professional covers about 40 articles of 2,500 words on GPT-6 Luna, 20 on Claude Sonnet 5 or GPT-6 Sol, and 13 on Claude Opus 5.5.

- Koala is still the better buy for built-in SEO data, Amazon affiliate roundups, and 100+ languages.

## Why People Look for Koala AI Alternatives

Koala packs a lot into one subscription. When people compare Koala AI alternatives, it's rarely because Koala does too little. It's about how the bill works, where autopilot starts, and fit.

### Words, not articles

Koala's plans are word allowances, and on 23 September 2026 it changed how they're counted. Its [pricing page](https://koala.sh/pricing) now says: "The word count is based on using GPT-6 Luna. GPT-6 Sol and Claude Sonnet 5 count words at 2x, and Claude Opus 5.5 counts at 3x." So the same plan writes a different number of articles depending on the model.

Here's what that means for articles of 2,500 words. This is our arithmetic, not Koala's: monthly words divided by 2,500 times the model's multiplier, rounded down.

| Koala plan | Billed monthly | Words a month | GPT-6 Luna (1x) | Sonnet 5 or GPT-6 Sol (2x) | Claude Opus 5.5 (3x) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $25 | 45,000 | 18 | 9 | 6 |
| Professional | $49 | 100,000 | 40 | 20 | 13 |
| Boost | $99 | 250,000 | 100 | 50 | 33 |

Treat those counts as estimates, since Koala sets length by [sections of about 400 words](https://support.koala.sh/en/article/how-to-limit-word-count-in-koalawriter-1xfkx2c/). In fairness, the same [changelog](https://koala.sh/changelog) update moved GPT-5.6 Sol users from 3x to 2x, which stretched their words further.

### Autopilot starts at $99

Koala's Content Calendar Autopilot plans your month. You approve the topics, and each article is "written and published on its scheduled date without you opening the app." It starts on the $99 Boost plan, which Koala's own [plans post](https://koala.sh/blog/brand-dna-plans) calls "the plan most people should start with." Below Boost, bulk-written articles upload to your site as drafts, with no calendar. Since the 28 August 2026 plan change, Professional's SEO agent is also capped at 50 messages a cycle.

### Six products in one bill

Koala sells "One subscription. Six products": the writer plus chat, images, internal links, lead magnets, and its Brand DNA agent. All but the writer draw on a separate credit pool, 1,000 a month on Professional. That's good value if you use them. If you only want articles, you pay for tools you may not open.

### Reviews are mixed

On [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/koala.sh), Koala had 3.2 out of 5 from 22 reviews when checked on 23 September 2026. The low reviews mention cancellation, recurring charges, fast credit use on premium models, and slow support. On [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/10011831/Koala-AI/reviews/), it rated 4.5 from 70 reviews on 21 September 2026, so plenty of users are happy.

## The 9 Best Koala AI Alternatives in 2026

### 1. Rankbox — best for a fixed 30 articles a month on autopilot

Of the Koala AI alternatives here, Rankbox is the one built around a fixed monthly count. We make it, so weigh this entry with that in mind. It costs $49.50 a month for 30 articles on one site, about $1.65 each. Autopilot is part of that one plan, and the count doesn't depend on which model wrote the article.

Rankbox reads your site and maps the questions your buyers ask Google and AI assistants, using its [answer-space research](/features/answer-space-research). Its [citation-ready writer](/features/citation-ready-writer) drafts each article from live research of the pages ranking now, at about 2,750 words, with sources cited inline and in a References list. Every draft is re-run against its failing SEO checks until it passes the [SEO and GEO score](/features/seo-geo-score). It writes on your weekly cadence, up to one article a day.

- **Price:** $49.50 a month for 30 articles, with a 7-day free trial of up to 7 articles. The trial takes a card, there's no yearly plan, and subscriptions are non-refundable. Extra sites cost $49.50 a month each.
- **Publishing:** articles reach your site through the [Rankbox publishing API](/integrations/api), which a developer sets up once. One-click plugins for WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, Framer, and Square are in development.

**Trade-offs:** Koala gives you more to work with. It has keyword, backlink, and SERP data and a Search Console agent, while Rankbox's demand figures are AI estimates. Koala also offers a choice of models, Amazon roundups, 100+ languages, and native WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, and Ghost publishing. Rankbox has no live AI citation monitoring yet and one login per account.

**Best for:** founders who want a set number of cited, SEO-checked articles on autopilot without moving up to a $99 plan. Our [Rankbox vs Koala AI comparison](/alternatives/koala-ai) goes row by row.

### 2. SEOWriting.ai — best like-for-like swap for affiliate sites

Among Koala AI alternatives, SEOWriting.ai is the closest match to KoalaWriter's one-click writer, and it appears on six of the twelve Koala lists we read. It reads the top 10 search results to set an outline, then writes the article in one click. It bulk-writes up to 100 articles in a batch and posts them to WordPress, and it's built for affiliates, with Amazon product lists and "20+ pre-trained models for affiliate content."

The main difference from Koala is billing. Its [homepage](https://seowriting.ai/) says that on Starter and Professional, "billing is based only on the number of generations, not on the word count." Articles run up to 5,000 words.

**Price:** Starter is [$19 a month](https://seowriting.ai/pricing), or $14 billed yearly, for up to 50 articles. Professional is $79, or $59 billed yearly, for up to 250, and adds internal and external linking. A free plan gives 3 articles without payment details, though its terms reserve the right to ask for billing information.

- WordPress publishing, scheduled or immediate, plus a Shopify app.
- A WordPress MCP server, so Claude can draft and publish posts.
- Models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta, DeepSeek, and Groq, in 48 languages.

**Trade-offs:** Starter articles go out without internal or external links. Native publishing covers WordPress and Shopify only, and there's no AI tracking. The plans say "up to" 50 articles, so check how many credits your article type uses. On [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/seowriting.ai), it showed 4.6 out of 5 from 365 reviews on 23 September 2026.

**Best for:** WordPress affiliate sites that want the lowest price per article.

### 3. Machined — best for topic clusters on a budget

Machined plans in clusters, not single articles, which sets it apart from other Koala AI alternatives. You enter a seed keyword, and it "automatically finds hundreds of related keywords, groups them by search intent," then writes a pillar page and supporting articles. Same-intent keywords go into one article, so two pages don't compete for one query, a problem called [keyword cannibalization](/glossary/keyword-cannibalization). The articles are linked to each other for you.

It also sidesteps Koala's model question. Machined's [model docs](https://machined.ai/docs/features/ai-model) say "All users have access to the same models regardless of plan or API mode." You pick OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or OpenRouter models per cluster, and plans count articles, not words.

**Price:** Launch is [$19 a month](https://machined.ai/pricing), or $14 billed yearly, for 30 articles. Growth is $49, or $37 billed yearly, for 100. A $99 plan lets you bring your own AI key, at "around $0.20 each in provider costs." The 7-day trial of Launch needs no card and "You won't be charged automatically." After that, "we do not offer refunds once a subscription has been charged."

- Every feature on every plan, including research, citations, images, and links.
- Unlimited websites, and 2 team members even on Launch. Koala and Rankbox each run on one login.
- Target lengths from 1,200 to 3,200 words, in 100+ languages.

**Trade-offs:** native publishing is [WordPress and Webflow](https://machined.ai/features/integrations); anything else goes through a webhook. Its pages describe batch publishing, live or as drafts, rather than a content calendar. There's no AI tracking, and some of its docs still describe older plans.

**Best for:** new sites building [topical authority](/glossary/topical-authority) one [topic cluster](/glossary/topic-cluster) at a time.

### 4. Junia AI — best for scheduled publishing under $99

If Koala's $99 entry point for autopilot is the problem, Junia AI is the lowest-priced scheduled option among these Koala AI alternatives. Its Scale Starter plan is [$59 a month](https://junia.ai/pricing) for "68 articles/month, generated and published on autopilot." You enter your site's URL, and AutoBlog writes and publishes on a schedule. The plan adds bulk generation, a content planner, auto Google indexing, team collaboration, API access, and unlimited websites.

It publishes natively to more places than Koala: WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Wix, Blogger, Ghost, and Medium, plus webhooks.

**Price:** Growth, for writing yourself, is $34 for 10 articles. Scale Starter is $59, Scale Plus $89 for 136 articles, and Scale Pro $159 for 283. Yearly prices are halved right now, to $29 a month for Scale Starter, by a "Limited time offer" with a countdown, so the regular yearly price isn't published. There's a free plan without a card, but its limits aren't shown.

**Trade-offs:** AutoBlog, bulk, and the API all need a Scale plan. There's no AI citation tracking, and its site gives two language counts, "150+" and "over 50." On [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/junia.ai), it showed 3.9 out of 5 from 88 reviews on 23 September 2026.

**Best for:** small sites that want articles published on a schedule, including to Wix, Blogger, or Ghost, for under $60 a month.

### 5. Autoblogging.ai — best for Amazon review and news sites

Among Koala AI alternatives, Autoblogging.ai comes closest to Koala's affiliate toolkit. It has an Amazon Reviews mode with "Real Amazon product data" and "Auto affiliate tag insertion," a News mode fed by Google News, YouTube-to-blog and RSS-to-blog tools, and bulk runs of "up to 500 articles at once via CSV upload."

Like Koala, it meters usage, but in credits. A Quick or Amazon Reviews article costs 1 credit, and a Godlike Mode article, which adds SERP competitor analysis, costs 2. Non-English articles cost 1 credit more, and its Fact Checker adds 2. Unused credits roll over while you stay subscribed. It also tracks visibility in Google's AI Overviews, with a few keywords free each month.

**Price:** [Starter](https://autoblogging.ai/) is $19 a month for 40 credits, Regular $49 for 120 credits and three sites, and Standard $99 for 300. Billed yearly, they drop to $12, $32, and $64 a month. By our arithmetic, Regular covers 30 Godlike articles with 60 credits to spare, or 60 articles at about $0.82 each. Free accounts get 10 credits a month without a card.

It publishes to [WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Webflow, Blogger, and Ghost](https://autoblogging.ai/feature/multi-platform-integrations), plus Medium, Tumblr, and other web 2.0 sites.

**Trade-offs:** credit costs stack up, so cost per article varies. The API starts at $249 a month, and AI tracking covers Google AI Overviews only. Some of its marketing, including a homepage testimonial about "parasite SEO," speaks to volume tactics that carry more risk for a brand's own site.

**Best for:** affiliate and news publishers who want Koala's content modes with credits that roll over.

### 6. Byword — best for programmatic SEO with fixed article credits

Byword skips the word meter: one credit is one article, whatever its length or model. Credits roll over and never expire. Of the Koala AI alternatives here, it's the one built for volume with a team. Its templates take variables filled from CSV, Google Sheets, or Airtable, so a spreadsheet becomes hundreds of pages. That's [programmatic SEO](/glossary/programmatic-seo).

Byword publishes to WordPress, Webflow, Ghost, HubSpot, Medium, and GoHighLevel, and a scheduler spreads finished articles over days. It's candid about the split, too. Its own [Koala comparison](https://byword.ai/compare/byword-vs/koala-writer/) credits Koala with the better Amazon affiliate tools.

**Price:** Starter is [$99 a month](https://byword.ai/pricing), or $990 a year, for 25 articles, with extras at $3.50 each. Standard is $299 for 80 articles, three seats with roles, and API access. Scale is $999 for 300. You start free with 5 articles and no card. Byword's [billing docs](https://byword.ai/learn/docs/account/billing/) say "We don't offer automatic refunds."

**Trade-offs:** it's one of the pricier options per article here, at $3.96 on Starter against $2.45 on Koala's Professional plan at 2x. There's no Amazon data, and its AI Search feature runs through Trakkr, a separate paid product. Its scheduler spreads out articles you've already generated; it doesn't pick topics by itself.

**Best for:** teams building programmatic pages who want each article's price fixed in advance. See [Koala AI vs Byword](/compare/koala-ai-vs-byword) for the head-to-head, or our [Byword alternatives](/blog/byword-alternatives) if Byword is on your list too.

### 7. ZimmWriter — best for no word caps

Among Koala AI alternatives, [ZimmWriter](https://zimmwriter.com/) takes the opposite approach to word allowances. It's a Windows desktop app, and "you bring your own API keys. No monthly word limits." You pay OpenAI or OpenRouter directly, or nothing for a local model through Ollama, and ZimmWriter charges a flat licence. Koala keeps a ZimmWriter page in its own [alternatives hub](https://koala.sh/alternatives), so the two often share a shortlist.

It's built for bulk. It writes up to 1,000 blog posts in one click, and up to 50 product roundups of 15 products each for the AAWP and AFFI.ai plugins. Its Local SEO Buffet builds up to 625 service-and-location pages. It connects up to 100 WordPress sites and schedules posts daily, weekly, or monthly.

**Price:** $24.97 a month, or $247 a year. Your AI bill is separate. ZimmWriter's own example: with GPT-4o Mini, "250,000 words costs approximately a quarter." Premium models cost more. Live SERP data and Amazon product details need ScrapeOwl, at $5 a month.

**Trade-offs:** it runs on Windows and publishes natively only to WordPress. You manage API accounts, rate limits, and costs yourself. It makes no AI search claims, and its homepage lists no trial or refund terms.

**Best for:** experienced affiliate and local SEO operators who want per-section control and no word caps.

## Which Koala AI Alternative Should You Choose?

The right pick among Koala AI alternatives depends on why you're leaving. Match the reason to the tool.

- **You want a fixed number of articles on autopilot for under $99:** Rankbox, at $49.50 for 30.
- **You want the lowest price per article on WordPress:** SEOWriting.ai or Machined.
- **You want scheduled publishing to Wix, Webflow, Ghost, or Blogger:** Junia AI.
- **You run Amazon affiliate or news sites:** Autoblogging.ai, or SEOWriting.ai for product lists.
- **You build pages from a spreadsheet, with a team:** Byword.
- **You want no word caps and full control, on Windows:** ZimmWriter.
- **You already pay for Semrush data:** Semrush Content Toolkit.
- **You want AI visibility tracking in the same bill:** Scalenut.

One rule applies to all Koala AI alternatives, because most sell volume: 100 articles in a batch, 500 from a CSV, 1,000 in one click. Google says "Appropriate use of AI or automation is not against our guidelines," but it draws the line at [scaled content abuse](/glossary/scaled-content-abuse), "when many pages are generated for the primary purpose of manipulating search rankings and not helping users." Publish only pages that answer a real question, cite sources, and add something new. Thirty of those beat three hundred template pages, and [keyword difficulty](/glossary/keyword-difficulty) matters more than volume.

## When Koala AI Is Still the Right Choice

Koala does several things better than most Koala AI alternatives here. It's worth staying if these describe you.

1. **You want SEO data and an agent in one bill.** Koala's [SEO agent](https://koala.sh/seo-agent) plans from your Search Console and Analytics, with "Keyword volumes, difficulty, SERPs, backlinks and competitor rankings, included in your plan." Rankbox estimates demand with AI instead.
2. **You write Amazon roundups.** Koala builds product roundups and single-product reviews from [live Amazon data](https://koala.sh/features/amazon-affiliate-articles), from the $25 Starter plan.
3. **You publish in many languages or for many brands.** Koala writes in [100+ languages](https://koala.sh/features/multilingual-support) with country targeting, and its plans run to 75 brands and 10 million words.
4. **You want to fix pages you already have.** [KoalaLinks](https://koala.sh/links) adds internal links, related posts, and schema to existing pages on any CMS.
5. **You write on GPT-6 Luna and don't need a calendar.** At $49 for about 40 articles, Professional costs less per article than Rankbox.

If one of those decides it, Koala is money well spent. Our [Rankbox vs Koala AI page](/alternatives/koala-ai) lays out the rest side by side.

## How to Switch From Koala AI

Moving to one of these Koala AI alternatives is low-risk if you go in order. Your published articles live on your own site, so they stay put.

1. **Pull your Search Console data first.** Note which Koala articles get clicks. Those are the pages to protect.
2. **Keep every URL that ranks.** If you must change one, set up a 301 redirect. Our [redirect generator](/tools/redirect-generator) writes the rules.
3. **Map your KoalaLinks pages.** Koala says those links keep working after you cancel, so note which pages use them before a new tool adds [internal links](/glossary/internal-linking) too.
4. **Use what you've paid for.** Yearly plans get the year's words up front, and they last 12 months. On monthly plans, credits expire after a month.
5. **Test within the refund terms.** Koala refunds within 15 days if you've used under 15,000 words and 100 credits. Machined and Byword don't offer routine refunds, and Rankbox's subscriptions are non-refundable, so test in each trial.
6. **Point the new tool at topics Koala hasn't covered.** Two articles on the same query compete with each other.
7. **Review before you go hands-free.** Approve the first month's articles yourself.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best Koala AI alternative?

It depends on why you're leaving. For a fixed 30 articles a month on autopilot, Rankbox costs $49.50. For the lowest price per article, SEOWriting.ai and Machined start at $19 a month. For Amazon review and news sites, Autoblogging.ai is the closest match to Koala's affiliate tools.

### Is there a cheaper alternative to Koala AI?

Yes. SEOWriting.ai lists up to 50 articles for $19 a month and Machined 30 for $19, against Koala's $25 Starter plan for 45,000 words. Koala itself is cheap per article on GPT-6 Luna, about $1.23 on Professional by our arithmetic, but not on Claude Opus 5.5.

### Are there free Koala AI alternatives?

None is free for ongoing use at volume, but several Koala AI alternatives let you start free, without a card. SEOWriting.ai gives 3 free articles, Byword 5, and Autoblogging.ai 10 credits a month. Junia AI has a free plan, and Machined a 7-day trial. Koala itself gives new accounts 5,000 words and 25 credits.

### How many articles do Koala AI's word plans cover?

By our arithmetic, for 2,500-word articles, Professional's 100,000 words cover about 40 on GPT-6 Luna, 20 on Claude Sonnet 5 or GPT-6 Sol, and 13 on Claude Opus 5.5. Boost's 250,000 words cover about 100, 50, or 33. Your count depends on article length and model.

### Is Koala AI worth it in 2026?

It can be. Koala is worth it if you use its SEO data, Amazon roundups, 100+ languages, or KoalaLinks, or if you write on GPT-6 Luna. If you only want a fixed number of articles published on a schedule, several Koala AI alternatives do that job for less than Boost's $99.
