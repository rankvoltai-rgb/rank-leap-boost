The best Writesonic alternatives in 2026 depend on which half of Writesonic you used. For articles, Rankbox writes 30 cited articles a month for $49.50, Frase pairs a writer with AI tracking from $49, and Jasper suits marketing copy across channels. For AI visibility tracking, Otterly.AI starts at $29 a month, Peec AI suits teams at $95, and AthenaHQ and Profound add content agents on top of the tracking.

Writesonic launched in 2021 as an AI writing tool. Today its homepage calls it "The AI Search Growth Engine," and its plans are built around tracked prompts, with 15 to 50 AI articles a month on top. So most people who search for Writesonic alternatives want one of two things: a better writer, or a tracker without the bundle.

Every price below was checked on 23 September 2026, on each vendor's own pricing page. We make Rankbox, so it's listed first, and it covers the writing side only. We've been just as plain about where Writesonic is still the better buy.

- Name the job first. If you used Writesonic to write, look at picks 1 to 4. If you used it to track AI answers, look at picks 5 to 10.

- Profound no longer sells a self-serve plan to brands. Lists that quote it at $99 a month are out of date.

## Why People Look for Writesonic Alternatives

Writesonic is a capable product. On [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/writesonic.com) it had 4.6 out of 5 from 5,764 reviews when checked on 21 September 2026. People compare Writesonic alternatives mostly because the product changed shape, not because it stopped working. These reasons come up most.

### It isn't the writer many reviews describe

In a July 2026 post, Writesonic's founder wrote: "We launched Writesonic in 2021 as an AI writing tool." Today its pricing page is titled "Writesonic AI Search Visibility Platform." Many 2026 roundups still describe credit-based writing plans or a $16 Chatsonic plan, and neither is on the current pricing page. If you came for a writer, you now buy a tracker with a writer attached.

### Articles are now a monthly quota

Starter includes 15 AI articles a month, Basic 25, and Growth 50. Writesonic's own help-center plan page, last updated 13 March 2026, still lists 100 articles on Basic, so check which page you're reading. At $99 for 15 articles, Starter works out at $6.60 an article on monthly billing, by our arithmetic. To be fair, that price also buys prompt tracking, site audits, and AI crawler analytics, and Writesonic says its articles run 3,000 to 8,000 words.

### Three engines on every self-serve plan

Writesonic's homepage says it tracks 10 AI platforms. Its self-serve plans track three: ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews. Perplexity, Claude, Copilot, Grok, and Google AI Mode sit on Enterprise. Sentiment starts at Growth, which is $499 a month. The Action Center, which turns findings into tasks, is a trial on Growth and only in full on Enterprise.

### You pay for both halves

Each plan bundles tracking and articles. If you only want [AI visibility](/glossary/ai-visibility) data, you pay for articles you may not use. If you only want articles, much of the price goes to tracking. Splitting the job between two cheaper tools can cost less, as the price section below shows.

### Nothing publishes on its own

Writesonic can push an article to WordPress when you choose. Its AI Article Writer page also names Webflow, Ghost, Sanity, and Contentful, though the plan table lists only WordPress on self-serve plans. Its Agents docs say "Nothing is published automatically, ever." That's a sensible default for teams with editors. It's a poor fit if you wanted articles to arrive on a schedule with nobody pressing a button.

## The 10 Best Writesonic Alternatives in 2026

Among these Writesonic alternatives, picks 1 to 4 are for writing and picks 5 to 10 are for tracking. Frase, Surfer SEO, and AthenaHQ sit across both.

### 1. Rankbox — best for cited articles on a schedule

Among Writesonic alternatives, Rankbox covers the content side only. We make it, so weigh this entry with that in mind. It plans, writes, and delivers long-form articles in the answer-first shape that AI answers tend to quote. It costs $49.50 a month for 30 articles, about $1.65 each. Writesonic Starter costs $99 for 15.

Here's the part that matters for this list: Rankbox does not monitor AI citations. It won't tell you whether ChatGPT named you this week. Instead, it grades every article for AI-answer readiness before delivery. If you need tracking, pair Rankbox with a tracker below. Otterly.AI Lite adds 15 prompts in four engines for $29, a total of $78.50 a month. Peec AI adds 50 prompts on three models for $95.

Rankbox starts by reading your site and mapping the questions your buyers ask Google and AI assistants, using its [answer-space research](/features/answer-space-research). Its [citation-ready writer](/features/citation-ready-writer) drafts each article from live research of the pages ranking now, at about 2,750 words, and cites its sources inline and in a References list. Each draft is re-run against its failing checks until it passes the [SEO and GEO score](/features/seo-geo-score). Every article opens with a direct answer and ends with an FAQ.

- **Price:** $49.50 a month for 30 articles on one site, with a 7-day free trial that takes a card. Extra sites cost $49.50 a month each.
- **Publishing:** articles reach your site through the [Rankbox publishing API](/integrations/api), which a developer sets up once. Plugins for WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, Framer, and Square are in development.
- **Cadence:** it writes on the weekly schedule you set, up to one article a day.
- **Research in your AI tools:** its research tools work inside 49 AI tools, including Claude, ChatGPT, and Cursor, through one MCP link.
- **Links and Reddit:** paid plans add a backlink exchange and Reddit reply drafts that you post yourself. Both are rolling out as of September 2026.

**Trade-offs:** no AI citation monitoring today. Keyword demand and difficulty are AI estimates, not keyword-database figures like the volume and difficulty Writesonic shows. There's one login per account and no native CMS plugin yet. Subscriptions are non-refundable, so use the trial to judge it.

**Best for:** founders and small teams who used Writesonic for articles and want twice the output for half the monthly price. Our [Rankbox vs Writesonic comparison](/alternatives/writesonic) goes row by row.

### 2. Frase — best for writing and tracking in one tool

Frase is the closest like-for-like swap among Writesonic alternatives, because it does both jobs. It was rebuilt from scratch in February 2026 as an SEO and GEO content platform. It researches a topic, writes the article, scores it for SEO, GEO, and EEAT, and publishes it. Its AI Visibility tracker checks your prompts daily and sends alerts.

Against Writesonic Starter, Frase Starter costs about half. At [$49 a month](https://www.frase.io/pricing), or $39 billed yearly, you get 10 articles, 50 audit pages, and 50 tracked prompts on ChatGPT and Google AI, for one seat and one site. Writesonic Starter gives you five more articles and adds Gemini. Frase's agent can draft and auto-publish on every plan, to WordPress, Webflow, Sanity, Wix, or Frase's own hosted CMS.

**Price:** Starter $49, or $39 billed yearly. Professional is $129, or $103 billed yearly, for 40 articles, five sites, three seats, and Perplexity tracking. Scale is $299, or $239 billed yearly, and adds Claude and Gemini. The 7-day free trial doesn't need a card.

**Trade-offs:** Starter tracks two engines and leaves out the content calendar and internal-link suggestions. Scheduled publishing works with WordPress and Frase's own CMS only. Its terms say "All fees paid are final," so treat the trial as your test.

**Best for:** small teams who want both jobs in one tool for about half of Writesonic's entry price. If Frase is on your shortlist too, see our [Frase alternatives](/blog/frase-alternatives).

### 3. Surfer SEO — best for teams that edit every draft

Surfer SEO ties Jasper as the most-listed of the Writesonic alternatives we read, on 10 of the 18 lists. Its Content Editor grades a draft against the pages ranking now, with one Content Score that combines SEO and AI Search. Surfer AI can write a full draft inside the editor. Positive Group bought Surfer in October 2025.

The fit differs from Writesonic's. Surfer assumes a person works each draft. Its WordPress plugin sends new posts as drafts by default, and its docs describe no built-in scheduling. Tracking comes two ways. Standard, at [$119 a month](https://surferseo.com/pricing/) or $99 billed yearly, tracks 25 ChatGPT prompts weekly, alongside 30 Documents a month and three seats. Or buy AI Search Analytics alone, for $95 a month or $82 billed yearly. That gets you 50 prompts refreshed daily across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Mode, and AI Overviews, with five seats.

**Price:** Discovery $59 with no AI tracking, Standard $119, Pro $219 with 50 daily prompts in all five engines, and Peace of Mind $359. The 7-day trial gives you Pro, takes a card, and turns into a paid plan unless you cancel.

**Trade-offs:** Claude isn't in its tracked-engine list. Internal linking starts at Pro. Each AI article uses one Document from your plan.

**Best for:** content teams of three or more who edit every draft and want tracking next to the editor. Compare it with Frase in [Surfer SEO vs Frase](/compare/surfer-seo-vs-frase).

### 4. Jasper — best for marketing copy across channels

If you used Writesonic for ads, emails, and social posts as much as blog posts, Jasper is the closest of the Writesonic alternatives in spirit. It calls itself a marketing agents platform. It offers more than 100 agents for email, social, ads, and landing pages, and every agent follows the brand voice you set.

Its Pro plan is [$69 a month per seat](https://www.jasper.ai/pricing), or $59 billed yearly. Pro is capped at one seat and includes two Brand Voices, five knowledge assets, and three audiences. There's no published article quota on Pro. A fair-use policy applies instead.

**Price:** Pro $69, or $59 billed yearly. Business is custom, starts with a 12-month commitment, and adds credits for advanced agents. The 7-day Pro trial needs a card and turns into a paid plan.

**Trade-offs:** AI visibility tracking lives in GEO Hub, which is Business-only and billed in credits. No native WordPress publishing is documented, and Webflow publishing is a Business beta. Keyword data comes from a Semrush integration, which needs its own Semrush plan.

**Best for:** marketing teams who need on-brand copy in every channel. Our [Jasper vs Writesonic](/compare/jasper-vs-writesonic) page compares the two in seven rounds.

### 5. Otterly.AI — best for the lowest tracking price

Otterly.AI is the cheapest established tracker among these Writesonic alternatives. It calls itself an AI search monitoring tool. It tracks brand mentions and website citations, and it audits pages for GEO. Its Lite plan is [$29 a month](https://otterly.ai/pricing), or $25 billed yearly, for 15 prompts tracked daily in four engines: ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Microsoft Copilot.

That engine list is the point. Writesonic keeps Perplexity and Copilot for Enterprise, and Otterly.AI includes both from $29, with unlimited team members. Google AI Mode and Gemini cost $9 a month each on Lite, and Claude costs $29. Every plan tracks in 50+ countries.

**Price:** Lite $29, or $25 billed yearly. Standard is $189, or $160, for 100 prompts. Premium is $489, or $422, for 400. The 7-day trial doesn't need a card.

**Trade-offs:** 15 prompts is a spot-check, not a full program, and Lite caps recommendations at three a week. API and MCP access start at Standard. Writing and publishing aren't its focus.

**Best for:** small teams who want to watch a handful of key prompts cheaply, or anyone pairing a tracker with a writer.

### 6. Peec AI — best for tracking for a whole team

Peec AI is a pure tracker, and says so: it "does not write or publish content itself." On the tracking side of Writesonic alternatives, it's the closest to Writesonic's own dashboards, without the article quota. Its Starter plan is [$95 a month](https://peec.ai/pricing), or $80 billed yearly, for 50 prompts on three models you choose. **Price:** Starter $95, or $80 billed yearly. Pro is $245, or $205, for 150 prompts. Advanced is $495, or $420, for 350. Agency plans start at $245. There's a free trial, and its length isn't stated on the pricing page.

**Trade-offs:** self-serve plans track three models, and Claude, Grok, and DeepSeek need Enterprise. Its pricing page lists Perplexity as an add-on, while another Peec page says every plan includes it, so confirm before you buy. Prompt volume is a relative score from 1 to 5, not a count.

**Best for:** in-house teams and agencies that want clean tracking with no seat limits. Our [Profound vs Peec AI](/compare/profound-vs-peec-ai) comparison covers it in seven rounds.

### 8. Ahrefs Brand Radar — best for teams already on Ahrefs

Ahrefs Brand Radar comes at AI visibility from the data side. Its AI Visibility Index runs over 451 million prompts, built from real search demand, across six AI platforms. So you can look up any brand's AI visibility with no setup. That index costs $199 a month per platform, or $699 for all of them.

For your own prompts, the maths is friendlier. Every paid Ahrefs plan includes daily custom prompts: 5 on Lite at $129 a month, 10 on Standard, and 20 on Advanced. So $50 covers about 83 prompts a day on one platform, or about 27 across three.

**Price:** custom prompts from $50 a month, the AI Visibility Index at $199 per platform or $699 for all, and included prompts on Ahrefs plans from $129. Brand Radar can be bought on its own, and it comes with a free Ahrefs account.

**Trade-offs:** index data for chatbots refreshes monthly, and only custom prompts run daily. Ahrefs says the index "may have limited coverage" for brands with little search demand. Claude uses eight checks per update. Writing isn't its focus.

**Best for:** Ahrefs users, and established brands that want to benchmark against competitors at scale.

**Price:** Essential is free. Starter is $295, or $245 billed yearly. Enterprise is custom. API access and extra credits cost more on Starter.

**Trade-offs:** $295 is about three times Writesonic Starter's price. Credits mean your cost depends on prompts, models, and how often you run them. Starter covers a single region and language, and its pricing page doesn't make clear which integrations each plan includes.

## How to Switch From Writesonic

Moving to one of these Writesonic alternatives is low-risk if you do it in order. Articles you've already published live on your own site, so they stay put.

1. **Save your prompt list and a snapshot of results.** Don't assume tracking history will move with you. Record where you stand today, so your new tracker has a baseline.
2. **Pull your Search Console data.** Note which Writesonic articles get impressions and clicks. Those are the pages to protect.
3. **Keep every URL that ranks.** If you must change one, set up a 301 redirect. Our [redirect generator](/tools/redirect-generator) writes the rules.
4. **Run the new tool's trial before Writesonic renews.** Writesonic says cancellations take effect at the next billing cycle, so line up your trial with your renewal date.
5. **Be careful with yearly plans.** Writesonic's yearly billing is paid 12 months up front. Try any replacement on monthly billing first, as our guide to [cheap SEO](/blog/cheap-seo) advises.
6. **Re-create your prompts word for word.** The same wording keeps your before-and-after numbers comparable.
7. **Point your writer at new questions.** Two articles that target the same query compete with each other, a problem called [keyword cannibalization](/glossary/keyword-cannibalization).
