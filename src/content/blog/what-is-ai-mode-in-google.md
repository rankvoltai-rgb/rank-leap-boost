AI Mode in Google is a chat-style way to search: you ask a full question, get one AI-written answer with links, and keep going with follow-up questions. To open it, tap the AI Mode button by the search bar on google.com, go straight to google.com/ai, tap AI Mode in the Google app, or click AI Mode in Chrome's address bar.

It's free, and it's widely available. As of September 2026, Google's [AI Mode help page](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/16011537?hl=en) lists 248 countries and territories and 98 languages. A few extras, such as booking, buying and the Deep Search research tool, are limited to certain countries, ages or paid plans.

This is the practical user's guide: where the button is on each device, who can use which features, what you can ask it to do, and how to get back to plain results. For what happens under the hood, read [what Google AI Mode is and how it works](/blog/what-is-google-ai-mode). If you run a website, our [guide to Google AI Mode and web traffic](/blog/google-ai-mode-vs-traditional-search) covers what it means for your clicks.

## Key Takeaways

- There are six doors into AI Mode in Google: the AI Mode button on google.com, the google.com/ai address, the Google app, Chrome's address bar, the Google app for Windows, and a follow-up typed under an AI Overview.
- Google lists 248 countries and territories and 98 languages for AI Mode as of September 2026. Buying is US-only, and several other tasks are English-only for signed-in adults.
- You can ask by typing, speaking, adding photos or files, pasting a link, or talking live with your camera through Search Live in the Google app.
- Google's help pages describe no setting that turns AI Mode off. It opens when you pick it or follow up on an AI Overview, and tapping All takes you back to regular results.
- With Search history on, your threads are saved to AI Mode history, where you can reopen them or delete one or all.

## Where to Find AI Mode in Google

Google's help page names [three ways in](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/16011537?hl=en): the google.com/ai address, the AI Mode button after you type a question on google.com, and the AI Mode button on the Google app's home screen. Chrome, the Windows app and AI Overviews add three more. We've put them all into one table, the **AI Mode Access Matrix**, with Chrome split into its two entry points.

| Where you are                     | How to open AI Mode                                                    | Useful extra                                                   |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| google.com in any browser         | Type a question, then tap AI Mode                                      | Tap All to switch back to regular results                      |
| Any browser, direct               | Go to google.com/ai                                                    | Bookmark it to skip a step                                     |
| Google app on Android or iOS      | Tap AI Mode on the home screen                                         | Lens and Live are one tap away                                 |
| Chrome on a computer              | Click AI Mode at the right of the address bar, or press Tab then Enter | Ask about the page you're on; the answer opens in a side panel |
| Chrome's New Tab page             | Click AI Mode beside the search box                                    | Add open tabs, images or files to your question                |
| Google app for Windows            | Press Alt + Space, then use AI Mode                                    | Ask about what's on your screen                                |
| An AI Overview in regular results | Type a follow-up under the Overview                                    | Your first question comes along for context                    |

### On google.com and google.com/ai

On the Google homepage, type your question in the search bar, then tap AI Mode. Your question goes to AI Mode in Google instead of the regular results page. The direct address, google.com/ai, skips that step and opens AI Mode straight away. On phones, Google's help says you can tap **All** at the top of the screen to go back and forth between the two.

### In Chrome and on Windows

Chrome's help page on [using AI Mode in Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/16704170?hl=en) shows an AI Mode button at the right of the address bar and on the New Tab page. The keyboard shortcut is Tab, then Enter. From there you can add recent tabs, upload an image or file, choose a model, or ask about the page you're reading. Google notes the Chrome button "is not available to everyone or on every device" yet, and is still reaching people outside the US and in other languages.

On a Windows 10 or later PC, the [Google app for desktop](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/16577498?hl=en) opens with Alt + Space and includes AI Mode. Google says it isn't supported yet for every account, country and language.

## Who Can Use AI Mode in Google: Countries, Languages and Accounts

Google's help page lists 248 countries and territories. That includes the US, Canada and Latin America, the UK and EU members, India, Japan, South Korea, Australia and most of Africa and the Middle East. Mainland China, Iran, Cuba and North Korea aren't on the list. The page also lists 98 languages, from Afrikaans to Zulu, including Hindi, Arabic, Japanese, Brazilian Portuguese and Spanish.

The core experience needs no subscription. At I/O in May 2026, Google made its newest default model available in AI Mode ["for everyone globally"](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/search-io-2026/). Some features are narrower, and the table shows who gets each one as of September 2026.

| Feature                              | Who can use it                                                        |
| ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| AI Mode itself                       | 248 countries and territories, 98 languages                           |
| Search Live (voice and camera)       | Google app on Android and iOS, wherever AI Mode works                 |
| Personal Intelligence                | Ages 18+, with history turned on; no subscription needed              |
| The "Pro" model                      | Signed-in users in English in listed countries, with daily limits     |
| Deep Search                          | Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers                                   |
| Price checks and calls to businesses | English, signed in, 18+                                               |
| Restaurant bookings                  | US, UK, Canada, Australia, India and a few more, in English and Hindi |
| Buying with Google Pay               | US only, English, signed in, 18+                                      |
| AI Mode experiment in Search Labs    | US and India, in English, ages 13+                                    |

A note on work and school accounts: if your Workspace admin has switched off the Search and Assistant service, Search history isn't available. You can still use AI Mode, but you can't save threads to pick up later.

## How to Use AI Mode in Google

Your first search takes about a minute. Here's the routine:

1. **Open AI Mode** using any door from the matrix above.
2. **Ask the whole question at once.** Add the details you'd normally spread across several searches: "Plan three days in Lisbon in March for two adults who love food markets, under €600." Google says the average AI Mode search in the US is [three times longer](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/ai-mode-us-insights/) than a regular one.
3. **Read the answer and open the links.** Since [May 2026](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/explore-web-generative-ai-search/), sources sit next to the sentences they support, and many answers end with suggestions for further reading.
4. **Ask a follow-up** in the "Ask anything" bar at the bottom. You don't need to repeat yourself: "Swap the second day for a trip to Sintra" works.
5. **Come back later** through AI Mode history, on the left side of the page on a computer.

### Ask with your voice, a photo, a file or a link

The "Ask anything" bar takes more than text. Tap the microphone to speak. Tap the menu, or Lens on a phone, to add one or more photos, or drag and paste them in on a computer. You can also upload PDFs, local files and Google Drive files, or paste a web address and ask a question about that page. Google's [one-year AI Mode report](https://storage.googleapis.com/gweb-uniblog-publish-prod/documents/AI-Mode-US-Insights.pdf) says more than one in six AI Mode searches in the US are now non-text, such as voice or images.

### Talk it through with Search Live

[Search Live](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/16329036?hl=en) is AI Mode by voice. In the Google app on Android or iOS, tap **Live** below the search bar and start talking. Tap **Video** to show Search what your camera sees, **Wave** to interrupt it mid-answer, and **Exit** to end. You can also jump from a typed AI Mode thread into Live at any point. Our post on [AI-powered voice search](/blog/voice-search-ai-powered) compares Search Live with the other voice assistants.

### Let it check prices, call or book

For errands, AI Mode in Google can do some legwork. Ask for something like "deep cleaning for an apartment move-out this Saturday," then choose **Check for me**. Google says AI Mode may [call businesses on your behalf](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/17104441?hl=en) when details aren't online, and it shares only what you confirmed. Type "don't call" if you'd rather it stuck to the web. In the US, some product listings also show a **Buy** button that [checks out with Google Pay](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/16833721?hl=en) at eligible retailers.

### Keep a project in a notebook

If you research one topic over weeks, a [notebook](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/17499802?hl=en) keeps its sources and instructions in one place. Open AI Mode history on a computer and select **Add notebook**. Notebooks sync with the Gemini app, though chats in each stay separate.

## How to Leave, Avoid or Clear AI Mode

Google's help pages don't document a switch that turns AI Mode off. You rarely need one, because it opens when you choose it or type a follow-up under an AI Overview. These are the controls Google does document:

| If you want to                        | Do this                                                              |
| ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Get back to regular results           | Tap **All** at the top of the page                                   |
| See links only, with no AI summary    | Choose the **Web** filter after searching                            |
| Remove the AI Mode button from Chrome | Right-click the address bar and untick **Always show AI Mode**       |
| Delete one thread or all of them      | Open AI Mode history, then **More** and **Delete** or **Delete all** |
| Stop personal tailoring               | Disconnect Gmail, Photos or Calendar, or turn off history            |
| Stop AI Mode calling a business       | Type "don't call" in your request                                    |

Two details are worth knowing. First, deleted AI Mode threads can linger in My Activity for up to 24 hours before they're removed automatically. Second, if you move from an AI Overview into AI Mode with a follow-up, your history may show two entries: the first search and the AI Mode thread. Delete both to remove it fully.

AI Overviews are a separate feature. Google's [AI Overviews help page](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/14901683?hl=en) says they "cannot be turned off," but the Web filter shows text links without them.

## If You Run a Website

AI Mode in Google links to web pages inside its answers, so your site can earn visits from it. Google's [rules for inclusion](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) are short: the page must be indexed and eligible to show a snippet, with no extra technical requirements. Site owners who want out can use the [Search generative AI control](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16908024?hl=en) in Search Console, which applies to AI Mode and AI Overviews together.

Two reporting quirks matter. Search Console [counts each AI Mode follow-up](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7042828?hl=en) as a new query, and visits from AI Mode arrive in Google Analytics as [ordinary Organic Search](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/9756891?hl=en). ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is AI Mode in Google free?

Yes. AI Mode in Google is free to use, and its default model is available to everyone. Google keeps some extras for paid Google AI Pro and Ultra plans, including Deep Search, newer model choices and higher usage limits.

### How do I turn off AI Mode in Google?

Google doesn't document an off switch, but AI Mode opens only when you choose it or follow up on an AI Overview. Tap **All** to return to regular results, use the **Web** filter for plain links, or hide Chrome's AI Mode button by right-clicking the address bar and unticking **Always show AI Mode**.

### Why don't I see AI Mode in Google?

AI Mode may not support your country, language or account type yet. Check Google's availability list on the AI Mode help page. In Chrome, the address-bar button is still rolling out outside the US and in other languages, so try google.com/ai instead.

### Does AI Mode in Google save my conversations?

Yes, if Search history is on. Threads appear in AI Mode history, where you can reopen them or delete one or all. Without history, AI Mode still works, but you can't pick up old threads.

### Can I use AI Mode in Google with my voice?

Yes. Tap the microphone in the "Ask anything" bar to speak a question. For a full voice conversation, use Search Live in the Google app on Android or iOS, which can also use your camera.

### Can AI Mode in Google make calls or bookings for me?

Sometimes. In English, for signed-in adults, AI Mode can check prices and availability and may call businesses for you. Restaurant bookings work in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, India and a few other countries. Buying through Google Pay is US-only.
