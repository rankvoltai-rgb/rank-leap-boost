Perplexity names a brand when the passages it retrieves for a question name that brand. For category questions, those passages come mostly from lists, reviews, marketplaces and community threads. For questions about one brand, its own site and profiles join them. So most brand mentions in Perplexity are earned on pages the brand doesn't own, and a mention doesn't need a link to the brand's site.

That makes a name and a link two different results. A review roundup can put your name in the answer while the citation goes to the roundup. Your blog can be cited for a general fact while your name never appears. Our [guide to getting cited by Perplexity AI](/blog/get-cited-by-perplexity) explains the retrieval layer behind both. This post stays on the names: where they come from, how shopping and Comet change who gets named, and how to read the gap between named and cited.

## Key Takeaways

- Brand mentions in Perplexity follow its sources. Highlighting any sentence shows the pages it came from.
- Lists name many brands at once. In a 2026 B2B study, Perplexity named the tracked brand in 41.7% of answers, against 32.3% for ChatGPT, and the authors credit list-heavy citations.
- Marketplaces and review sites matter for product questions. In Ahrefs' September 2026 data, six retail sites, including Amazon and Target, took 9.1% of the top-50 citation share between them, and ten review and testing sites took 9.5%.
- A brand can be named without being cited, and cited without being named. Track the two separately.
- Product cards aren't sponsored. Perplexity says it ranks product listings on "authority and relevance" and that richer product data raises the chance of being recommended.
- Brand names repeat more than sources do. In a 2026 study of repeat runs within 24 hours, Perplexity's named brands overlapped 0.49 and its sources 0.28, on a scale where 1 means identical.

## Where Brand Mentions in Perplexity Come From

Perplexity builds each answer from passages it retrieves, then cites them. Its own guide says you can ["highlight answer text to check the sources referenced."](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/getting-started) That makes brand mentions in Perplexity traceable. Pick any sentence that names a brand, highlight it, and you see which pages put the name there.

Two other inputs can add names. Perplexity's [memory](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/10968016-memory) can draw on a signed-in user's saved preferences and past searches, and it shows those references "in the sources." And a user can turn web search off for a thread, so the answer comes from the model alone. Both are exceptions. For a typical signed-out question, the names come from the cited pages.

### Category questions lean on lists, marketplaces and reviews

Ahrefs' [September 2026 snapshot](https://ahrefs.com/blog/most-cited-domains-perplexity/) counted every domain Perplexity cited across more than 3.1 million US queries on all topics. Reddit (21.6%) and YouTube (20.8%) led the top-50 share. Further down, two groups stand out for product questions:

- **Retail and marketplace sites:** Amazon 3.6%, Alibaba 2.0%, Target 1.3%, Home Depot 0.8%, Etsy 0.7% and Best Buy 0.7%, or 9.1% together.
- **Review, testing and rating sites:** Tripadvisor 1.5%, Consumer Reports 1.3%, TechRadar 1.1%, NerdWallet 1.1%, Yelp 0.9%, Trustpilot 0.8%, CNET 0.8%, PCMag 0.8%, RTINGS 0.6% and Tom's Guide 0.6%, or 9.5% together.

Each of those pages names several brands. When one is cited, every brand on it has a chance to be named.

Business software shows the same pattern. Analyze AI, a tracking vendor, [classified 115,843 citations](https://www.tryanalyze.ai/blog/state-of-ai-search-source-family-mix) from 460 B2B prompts by page type in August 2026. For Perplexity, 34.4% went to lists, comparisons and reviews, 35.4% to brand websites and 25.2% to editorial pages. In [Profound's data](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/ai-platform-citation-patterns) from August 2024 to June 2025, Gartner took 1.0% and G2 0.6% of all Perplexity citations.

### Questions about one brand lean on that brand's pages

For questions about a single brand, the brand's own site and profiles are the obvious sources. Yext's [October 2025 study](https://www.yext.com/blog/ai-citations-86-percent-of-sources-are-brand-managed) of 6.8 million citations across Gemini, OpenAI and Perplexity found 86% came from sources brands manage or influence: websites (44%), listings (42%) and reviews. Those figures pool three engines and mix branded and unbranded questions, so read them as a direction, not a Perplexity rate.

| Question type               | Pages that usually supply the names                | What decides whether you're named                |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| "Best X for Y"              | Lists, review sites, marketplaces, Reddit          | Whether the lists Perplexity cites include you   |
| "X vs Y"                    | Comparison pages, review sites, both brands' sites | Whether a fair comparison exists that covers you |
| "Alternatives to X"         | Rivals' alternatives pages, roundups, threads      | Whether you appear on the pages that answer it   |
| "What is X?" or "X pricing" | The brand's site, profiles, directories            | Whether your own pages state the facts plainly   |

If what Perplexity says about you by name is the problem, our [brand presence audit](/blog/brand-presence-in-perplexity) traces each wrong sentence to its source.

## The Name-and-Link Grid: Named but Not Cited, Cited but Not Named

We call this the Name-and-Link Grid. It treats brand mentions in Perplexity and citations of your site as two separate yes-or-no questions, so every answer lands in one of four cells. Each cell has its own cause and its own fix, which is why one "visibility score" hides more than it shows.

| Cell              | What you see                                     | Usual cause in Perplexity                       | What to do                                                                     |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Named and linked  | Your name in the text, your page in the sources  | Your page answered the question and named you   | Keep the page current and dated                                                |
| Named, not linked | Your name in the text, someone else's page cited | A list, review or thread mentioned you          | Publish your own page that answers the same question                           |
| Linked, not named | Your page cited, your name absent                | Your page supplied a general fact or definition | Fine for how-to questions; for buyer questions, tie the method to your product |
| Neither           | No name, no link                                 | You're missing from the pages Perplexity reads  | Get onto the lists it cites, and fix reachability                              |

### Why names outlast sources

The 2026 study ["Don't Measure Once"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07585) repeated the same prompts within 24 hours. Perplexity's cited sources overlapped by a Jaccard score of 0.282, where 1 means identical. Its named brands overlapped by 0.492. The same brand often survives a change of source, because several cited pages name it. That's good news for brands that appear on many lists, and bad news for brands that rely on one.

### How often each cell happens

On matched B2B prompts in the Analyze AI study, Perplexity named the tracked brand 41.7% of the time. The authors' explanation: when Perplexity cites a "top 10" article, all ten brands can reach the answer. Part of that rate is the named-not-linked cell at work.

The opposite cell is harder to size. As of October 2026, no published study we could check splits Perplexity's citations into named and unnamed. Semrush's [June 2026 study](https://www.semrush.com/blog/the-ghost-citations-study/) found 62% of citations were linked without the brand being named, but it covered ChatGPT, AI Overviews, Gemini and AI Mode only. Don't borrow its number for Perplexity. Count your own cells instead, as our guide to [tracking brand mentions in Perplexity](/blog/track-brand-mentions-in-perplexity) shows.

## How Shopping, Comet and Premium Sources Change Brand Exposure

Three features change brand mentions in Perplexity, either by adding new formats or by changing which brands get picked.

### Product cards and Instant Buy

For shopping questions, Perplexity shows product cards inside the answer. Its [Instant Buy help article](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/10352906-what-is-instant-buy) says it ranks product listings with criteria similar to its sources, "based on authority and relevance," and that it "analyzes product reviews across multiple sites." It adds three points that matter to brands:

1. **Data depth counts.** "Merchants who provide deeper product details such as availability, reviews, pricing, and specifications are more likely to be recommended."
2. **No paid slots.** "Advertisers cannot pay to list their products," and listings are set "based on relevance and ratings."
3. **Personal history counts.** Perplexity uses "past searches, interactions, and saved preferences" to refine recommendations, so a signed-in shopper may see different brands from a stranger.

Instant Buy is available to all US users, per the same article. Its [PayPal help page](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/12932923-instant-buy-buy-with-paypal) lists merchants such as Wayfair and "thousands of other merchants on BigCommerce, Shopware, and Wix platforms." Large retailers can also share product data through the free [Merchant Program](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/blog/shop-like-a-pro), which Perplexity says raises the chance of being a "recommended product."

### Comet

Comet is Perplexity's Chromium-based browser. Its [help center](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/11172798-getting-started-with-comet) lists Perplexity search as its primary search engine, plus Ask and Summarize buttons for the page a user has open. The [Comet page](https://www.perplexity.ai/comet) shows it on Mac, Windows, iOS and Android, with shopping among its assistant's jobs.

Two effects follow. More of a Comet user's searches run through Perplexity, so the same sourcing patterns apply to more of their questions. And when the assistant summarizes your own page, your brand is the page, so your on-page facts carry straight into the answer. Perplexity doesn't document how Comet's assistant chooses between merchants beyond the product-ranking rules above.

Publishers have a separate stake. [Comet Plus](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/blog/introducing-comet-plus) pays partner publishers on "human visits, search citations, and agent actions." It pays for being cited. It isn't described as changing which brands get named.

### Premium data sources

Perplexity lets users pick [premium sources](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/12870803-premium-data-sources) such as PitchBook, which offers "detailed firmographics for private and public companies," along with CB Insights and Statista. When a user picks one, company facts may come from that database instead of the open web. Keep your profiles there accurate if investors or analysts research you.

## Worked Example: Who Names Tallyfold?

Tallyfold is a fictional invoicing and payments app for agencies. Its rivals, Brindlework and Kestrelyn, are fictional too, and every number below is illustrative.

To map its brand mentions in Perplexity, Tallyfold runs 10 prompts three times each in incognito mode: five "best X" prompts, three comparisons and two questions about Tallyfold itself. That's 30 answers. It sorts each one into the grid:

| Cell              | Answers | Share    |
| ----------------- | ------- | -------- |
| Named and linked  | 5       | 17%      |
| Named, not linked | 7       | 23%      |
| Linked, not named | 4       | 13%      |
| Neither           | 14      | 47%      |
| **Total**         | **30**  | **100%** |

Tallyfold's mention rate is (5 + 7) ÷ 30 = 40%. Its citation rate is (5 + 4) ÷ 30 = 30%. The two numbers tell different stories, which is the point of keeping them apart.

Highlighting the seven named-not-linked sentences shows where the names came from: three from one review site's agency invoicing roundup, two from a Reddit thread, one from an app marketplace listing and one from a YouTube walkthrough (3 + 2 + 1 + 1 = 7). Tallyfold has no page of its own that answers "best invoicing software for agencies," so the roundup speaks for it.

The four linked-not-named answers all cite Tallyfold's guide to late-payment fees, used for a general fact in category answers. The guide never says how Tallyfold handles fees. The fixes follow the grid:

1. **Named, not linked:** publish a fair comparison of agency invoicing tools, and send the review site Tallyfold's current features and prices.
2. **Linked, not named:** add a short, factual section to the fees guide on how Tallyfold's reminder settings apply the method.
3. **Neither:** check the prompts with no Tallyfold presence against the lists Perplexity cites, and pitch the ones that fit.

## What Moves Brand Mentions in Perplexity

- **Be on the lists Perplexity cites.** Find them by highlighting named sentences in your category's answers.
- **Publish your own answer page** for each "best", "vs" and "alternatives" question buyers ask, with a dated comparison table.
- **Keep product and company data complete** wherever Perplexity reads it: product feeds, marketplaces and data providers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does Perplexity decide which brands to name?

It names the brands that appear in the passages it retrieves and cites for the question. For category questions those are mostly lists, reviews, marketplaces and threads. For questions about one brand, the brand's own pages and profiles weigh more. Perplexity says it ranks sources on "authority and relevance."

### Can I get brand mentions in Perplexity without my site being cited?

Yes. If a review roundup, marketplace listing or Reddit thread names you and Perplexity cites that page, your name can appear with no link to your site. In a 2026 B2B study, Perplexity's list-heavy citations helped it name tracked brands in 41.7% of answers.

### Why is my site cited but my brand not mentioned?

Your page probably supplied a general fact, such as a definition or a statistic, that didn't need your name. That's normal for how-to questions. For buyer questions, add a clear, factual line on how your product applies the method, so the passage carries your name.

### Are Perplexity product recommendations sponsored?

No. Perplexity's help center says advertisers can't pay to list products and that listings are ranked on relevance and ratings. It adds that merchants with fuller product details, such as availability, reviews, pricing and specifications, are more likely to be recommended.

### Does Comet change brand mentions in Perplexity?

Comet uses Perplexity as its main search engine, so the same sourcing applies to its users' searches. Its assistant can also summarize the page a user has open and help with shopping. Perplexity doesn't document how the assistant picks between merchants beyond its product-ranking rules.

### How often should I check brand mentions in Perplexity?

Monthly is enough for most brands, with a few runs per prompt. Sources rotate quickly, but named brands repeat more, so a monthly pooled count is steadier than any single answer.
