Yes, indirectly, and mentions matter too. Link building helps AI visibility mainly by getting your pages found, indexed and ranked by Google and Bing. Google's ranking feeds AI Overviews and AI Mode, Bing's feeds Copilot, and ChatGPT queries third-party search providers, Microsoft among them. Being named on other sites, with or without a link, also tracks closely with how often AI answers name you.

The case for link building rests on two kinds of evidence, and they carry different weight. Google and Microsoft document how links feed their search and AI systems. The rest comes from correlation studies, which show patterns but can't prove that more links cause more AI visibility.

This post sorts the evidence and turns it into a budget rule you can use. The idea of being named next to category leaders without a link, called co-citation, has its own guide: [our full guide to link building and co-citation for AI visibility](/blog/link-building-ai-visibility-co-citation). If you want the tools, see [AI link building without spam](/blog/ai-link-building).

## Key Takeaways

- Google says links help it find pages and judge relevance, that PageRank is still a core ranking system, and that its AI features are "rooted in" those same ranking systems.
- Microsoft says external links support "discovery, authority evaluation, and grounding eligibility" across Bing and Copilot.
- OpenAI names no link factor for ChatGPT search. Links reach it, if at all, through the search indexes it queries.
- Five large studies from 2025 disagree on details, but most point one way: link quality and brand mentions track AI visibility better than raw link counts.
- None of the studies is an experiment. Big brands win on links, mentions and AI answers at once, so treat every number as a correlation.
- Split your budget by your weakest stage. If you aren't indexed or ranking, fund links first. If you rank but AI answers don't name you, fund mentions.

## What Google and Microsoft Say Links Do

The strongest evidence that link building helps AI visibility is also the least exciting. It's the vendors' own description of how their search systems work. The table sums up what each one says, as of September 2026.

| Vendor         | What it documents about links                                                                          | What it says about its AI features                                                      |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Google         | Links are how it finds most new pages, a relevance signal, and part of PageRank, a core ranking system | AI Overviews and AI Mode use core ranking systems to retrieve pages                     |
| Microsoft Bing | External links from relevant sites help discovery; linking supports authority evaluation               | The same guidelines cover Copilot and grounding, and name linking as a grounding factor |
| OpenAI         | Nothing about links                                                                                    | ChatGPT ranks results with "multiple factors" and queries third-party search providers  |

### Google's chain, step by step

Google's [SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide) says most new pages it finds each day come through links. Its [ranking systems guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ranking-systems-guide) says PageRank "continues to be part of our core ranking systems." And its [guide to generative AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) says AI Overviews and AI Mode use "core Search ranking systems to retrieve relevant, up-to-date web pages."

Put those three statements together and link building helps a page get retrieved for Google's AI answers. Google has never said how much one link adds, so that last step is a reasonable inference, not a measured effect.

### Bing's statement is the most direct

Microsoft's [Bing Webmaster Guidelines](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/webmaster-guidelines-30fba23a) say "Strong internal and external linking supports discovery, authority evaluation, and grounding eligibility." Grounding is when Copilot or another AI product pulls web pages in to base its answer on. That's the only vendor sentence we know of that ties links to AI grounding by name.

### OpenAI stays quiet

OpenAI's [ChatGPT search help page](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9237897-chatgpt-search) says it "ranks search results using multiple factors intended to help users find relevant, reliable information." It doesn't list them. It does say ChatGPT sends rewritten queries to third-party search providers, and it links Microsoft's privacy statement among them. So for ChatGPT, links likely help through the indexes it queries, not through anything OpenAI has described. Our breakdown of [ChatGPT ranking factors](/blog/chatgpt-ranking-factors-ai-search-placement) grades the other signals.

## What Five Studies Found About Links and AI Visibility

Several SEO data companies have compared link metrics with AI visibility to see whether link building pays off in AI answers. Each one used a different sample and counted a different outcome, so read the "what it counted" column before the result.

| Study                                                                                                                        | Sample and method                                                                | What it counted                     | Link result                                                            | Mention result                                                               |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [SE Ranking](https://seranking.com/blog/how-to-optimize-for-chatgpt/), Nov 2025                                              | 129,000 domains, 216,524 pages, 20 niches; XGBoost model with SHAP               | Citations in ChatGPT answers        | Referring domains ranked first of all factors                          | Domains with heavy Quora and Reddit mentions averaged about 4x the citations |
| [Semrush with Growth Memo](https://www.semrush.com/blog/backlinks-ai-search-study/), Oct 2025                                | 1,000 random domains, five AI platforms; Pearson and Spearman                    | Brand mentions in AI answers        | Authority Score 0.65 (Pearson); nofollow links about equal to followed | Not measured                                                                 |
| [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-brand-correlation/), May 2025                                                   | 75,000 brands; Spearman                                                          | Brand mentions in AI Overviews      | Backlinks 0.218; referring domains 0.295                               | Branded web mentions 0.664                                                   |
| [Seer Interactive](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/what-drives-brand-mentions-in-ai-answers), Jan 2025              | 10,000 questions sent to GPT-4o through the API                                  | Brand mentions from the model alone | Backlinks "weak or even neutral"                                       | Google page-one rankings about 0.65                                          |
| [Search Atlas](https://searchatlas.com/blog/authority-metrics-in-the-age-of-llms-visibility-correlation-analysis/), Nov 2025 | 21,767 domains cited in ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, Aug to Oct 2025; Pearson | How prominent a cited domain was    | DR, DA and Domain Power slightly negative                              | Not measured                                                                 |

Ahrefs extended its study in [December 2025](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-brand-visibility-correlations/) to ChatGPT and AI Mode, with AI Overviews as a benchmark. Link counts stayed "very weak," while branded web mentions correlated 0.66 to 0.71 and YouTube mentions about 0.737.

### Where the studies agree

Three patterns hold across most of them.

1. **Quality beats quantity.** Raw link counts correlate weakly. Quality scores and the number of distinct linking domains do better.
2. **Mentions carry weight.** Both studies in the table that tested brand mentions, Ahrefs and SE Ranking, found them among the strongest signals. The exception is narrower: Seer found mentions on eight big news sites [barely tracked](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/does-being-mentioned-on-top-news-sites-impact-ai-answer-mentions) ChatGPT's recall, at about 0.07.
3. **Search ranking and AI visibility move together.** SE Ranking found pages with an average Google position of 1 to 45 averaged 5 ChatGPT citations, against 3.1 for positions 64 to 75. Seer found Google page-one rankings correlated about 0.65 with ChatGPT mentions.

### Where they part ways

SE Ranking crowns referring domains, while Ahrefs calls link metrics very weak. The gap mostly comes from what each counted. SE Ranking counted your URLs in the sources list, which rewards strong domains. Ahrefs counted your brand's name in the answer, which rewards brands people talk about. Search Atlas only looked at domains that were already cited, and asked which ones were most prominent. Our [co-citation guide](/blog/link-building-ai-visibility-co-citation) explains the split in more detail.

### What none of them can tell you

All five are observational, so none can show that link building itself causes AI visibility. A brand like Stripe has huge link counts, constant press and strong AI visibility, and none of those causes the others on its own. Semrush's own finding hints at this. Nofollow links, which Google says it [generally doesn't count for ranking](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2019/09/evolving-nofollow-new-ways-to-identify), correlated as strongly as followed ones. That fits "big brands earn every kind of link" better than "PageRank drives AI answers."

## How to Split a Link Building Budget for AI Visibility

The evidence points to a simple rule for link building budgets: fund the stage where you're weakest. We call it the Weakest-Stage Split. It's our framework, built from the patterns above, not a measured formula.

### The Weakest-Stage Split

| Your situation                                           | Weakest stage      | Suggested split: links / mentions             | Why                                                 |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Key pages aren't indexed in Google or Bing               | Discovery          | 70 / 30, after fixing crawl issues            | Engines can't retrieve what they haven't found      |
| Indexed, but you rank on page one for few category terms | Ranking            | 60 / 40                                       | Google and Bing both tie AI retrieval to ranking    |
| You rank, but AI answers rarely name you                 | Being talked about | 30 / 70                                       | Mentions track AI naming more closely than links do |
| AI answers name you with wrong facts                     | Accuracy           | 10 / 90, aimed at the sources with the errors | The fix is on the pages engines read                |

"Links" here means earned links: data studies, useful tools, partner pages and PR that editors choose to link. "Mentions" means getting named on the pages AI engines cite for your category, such as roundups, reviews and forums. The two overlap, since a good story often earns both.

To place yourself, check indexing in Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, check rankings for 10 category terms, and run a small prompt panel. Our [GEO measurement guide](/blog/how-to-measure-geo) shows how to size a panel. If answers get your facts wrong, start with [fixing incorrect brand facts in AI answers](/blog/fix-incorrect-brand-facts-in-ai-answers).

### Worked example: Tallyfold's $2,400 a month

Tallyfold is a fictional invoicing app for agencies, and these numbers are illustrative. It has $2,400 a month for off-site work. Its key pages are indexed, and it ranks on page one for 3 of its 10 category terms. In a 36-answer prompt panel, AI answers name it 4 times, an 11.1% visibility rate.

That's the "rank, but rarely named" row, so Tallyfold uses 30 / 70:

- **Links: 30% of $2,400 = $720.** It turns anonymized invoice data into a short study and pitches it to agency-finance writers who might link to the method.
- **Mentions: 70% of $2,400 = $1,680.** It sends fact sheets to editors of the roundups that AI engines cite, asks recent customers for honest reviews, and answers forum questions under staff names with disclosure.

Compare that with buying links. BuzzStream's [2026 study of guest post prices](https://www.buzzstream.com/blog/guest-post-costs/), across about 257,000 sites with usable data, put the average vendor price at $461. So $2,400 buys about five posts ($2,400 ÷ $461 = 5.2). The same study rated 96.2% of guest post sites low quality, and 39.6% had no organic traffic. And a paid link that passes ranking credit is link spam under Google's rules.

## What Not to Spend On

Some link building spend can hurt you in Google and Bing, which then hurts you in the AI features built on them. As of September 2026, Google's [spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) name these as link spam, among others:

- Buying or selling links that pass ranking credit, including with goods, services or free products.
- Excessive link exchanges and partner pages made just to cross-link.
- Automated programs or services that create links.
- Guest posts or press releases with keyword-optimized anchor links, placed across other sites.

Google allows paid links when they're marked `rel="sponsored"` or `rel="nofollow"`. Since its [December 2022 link spam update](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2022/12/december-22-link-spam-update), its SpamBrain system can detect "both sites buying links, and sites used for the purpose of passing outgoing links," and neutralize those links. Bing's guidelines add "private networks" and "artificial social promotion schemes that simulate popularity" to the list, and say these reduce grounding visibility in Copilot.

Mentions have a line too. Google's AI guide says "seeking inauthentic 'mentions' across the web isn't as helpful as it might seem." Fake reviews and planted forum posts don't count as earned mentions. The hub post covers the [full link spam policy and the safe version of each tactic](/blog/link-building-ai-visibility-co-citation).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do backlinks help you show up in ChatGPT?

Probably, but indirectly. OpenAI names no link factor, but ChatGPT queries third-party search providers, and Microsoft, one of them, says links feed Bing's authority evaluation. SE Ranking's study of 129,000 domains found referring domains were the strongest predictor of ChatGPT citations. That's a correlation, and it applies to citations of your URLs more than to your brand being named.

### Do nofollow links help AI visibility?

They correlate with it about as strongly as followed links. Semrush's October 2025 study of 1,000 domains found 0.340 for nofollow and 0.334 for followed links (Pearson). Google treats nofollow as a hint and generally doesn't count it for ranking, but the mention inside the link still reaches readers and AI models.

### Are brand mentions more important than backlinks for AI search?

For being named in AI answers, the data leans that way. Ahrefs found branded web mentions correlated 0.664 with AI Overview visibility, against 0.218 for backlinks. For getting your pages cited as sources, links held up better in SE Ranking's data. Build both, weighted by your weakest stage.

### Does link building help with Google AI Overviews?

Yes, through ranking. Google says AI Overviews and AI Mode use its core ranking systems to retrieve pages, and PageRank is one of those systems. A page that ranks well is more likely to be retrieved. Google hasn't published how much a single link changes that.

### Is buying backlinks worth it for AI search?

No. Google's spam policies call links bought for ranking "link spam," and SpamBrain neutralizes them. You can buy sponsored links for traffic or awareness if they're marked `rel="sponsored"`, but Google generally doesn't count those for ranking. BuzzStream's 2026 data also rated 96.2% of vendor guest post sites low quality.

### How long does link building take to affect AI answers?

Usually weeks to months, though no vendor publishes a timeline. A new link has to be crawled, then help a page rank, and only then can AI search retrieve it. What a model knows from training changes only with a new model. Measure with a fixed prompt panel before and after, so you can tell real change from noise.
