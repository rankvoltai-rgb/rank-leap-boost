You can track brand mentions in AI search for free. Run a short list of buyer prompts by hand in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Mode each week, log every answer in a spreadsheet, and add three free vendor reports: Bing's AI Performance report, Search Console's generative AI report and GA4's AI Assistant channel. Pay for a tracker when logging by hand costs more in hours than the plan costs, or when you need daily runs, several countries or a long history.

This page answers the money question. Our [full guide to tracking brand mentions in AI search](/blog/how-to-track-brand-mentions-in-ai-search) covers the whole system: the three prompt buckets, the recording template, cadence and alert rules. Here you get the free method, a simple test for when to pay, and a price table.

Before you pay anyone to watch brand mentions in AI search, know this: paid trackers don't see more of the engine than you do. Google's [AI optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) says "No third-party tool has access to our internal ranking or AI systems." Trackers run prompts the way you would, only more often. What you pay for is volume and time.

## Key Takeaways

- The free stack is a weekly prompt log plus Bing's AI Performance report, Search Console's generative AI report and GA4's AI Assistant channel.
- The three free reports cover Microsoft's and Google's AI features and your visits. For unlinked brand mentions in AI search, and for ChatGPT, Perplexity and Claude answers, your own log is the free record.
- Use the Tracking Break-Even Test: if a logged answer costs you about $1 of time, a $95 plan pays for itself at 95 answers a month.
- As listed on 28 September 2026, entry plans ran from $29 a month (Otterly.AI Lite) to $99 (Semrush, SE Visible and Chatbeat). Profound offers a free 7-day trial, then a custom contract.
- Free one-off checkers from Semrush, Ahrefs and HubSpot are snapshots. They show where you stand today, not how you're trending.

## The Free Method, Step by Step

Free tracking has two parts. You collect answers yourself, because no free report shows brand mentions in AI search across every engine. Then you add the vendor data that Microsoft and Google give away.

### A weekly log of brand mentions in AI search

1. **Write 15 to 20 buyer prompts.** Mix questions about your brand, your category ("best X for Y") and head-to-head comparisons. Keep your name out of the category prompts. The free [AI Visibility Prompt Kit](/tools/ai-visibility-prompt-generator) drafts 30 to choose from.
2. **Pick two or three engines** where your buyers ask. ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Mode are the usual start.
3. **Use a clean session.** Log out, or use a temporary chat without memory, and run from the same place each week.
4. **Log six fields per answer:** date, engine, prompt, named (yes or no), linked (yes or no), and the other brands and sites cited.
5. **Run each prompt once a week** and add up four weeks before you read a rate. Weekly swings on a small list are mostly noise.

That's 15 to 20 prompts times two or three engines, or 30 to 60 answers a week. The hub post has a fuller [recording template](/blog/how-to-track-brand-mentions-in-ai-search) if you want more columns later, and our glossary explains [prompt tracking](/glossary/prompt-tracking) in plain terms.

### Three free vendor reports

| Free report | What it counts | Where | What it leaves out |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bing Webmaster Tools, AI Performance | Citations of your pages, the grounding queries behind them, and your Citation Share | Copilot, Bing's AI summaries and select partners | Unlinked name mentions; competitor domains |
| Search Console, generative AI reports | Impressions of your URLs, by page, country, device and date | AI Overviews, AI Mode and Discover | Mentions of your name without your URL |
| GA4, AI Assistant channel | Sessions that arrive from AI assistants | ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Copilot, Grok | Answers nobody clicked; Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode |

Here is where each row comes from. Microsoft launched the [AI Performance report](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/February-2026/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview) in public preview on 10 February 2026 and added Citation Share in a [June update](https://blogs.bing.com/search/June-2026/New-AI-Visibility-Insights-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Intents-Topics-Citation-Share-Compare), noting it "does not expose competitor domains." Google's [generative AI performance reports](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/gen-ai-performance-reports) reached every site on 31 August 2026. GA4's [channel definition](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/9756891) names the five assistants in the table and excludes AI Overviews and AI Mode. Perplexity and Claude aren't named, so add a custom channel with our [15-minute GA4 setup](/blog/how-to-track-ai-referral-traffic-in-ga4).

Read all three by page once a month. A page that rises in Bing's citations and Google's AI impressions at the same time is one engines trust. Check whether your log shows it too.

### Free one-off checkers and trials

Free checkers are good for a first look, not for tracking over time:

- **Semrush's [AI Visibility Checker](https://www.semrush.com/free-tools/ai-search-visibility-checker/)** covers ChatGPT, Gemini, AI Mode and AI Overviews, and allows three checks a day without an account.
- **Ahrefs' [free AI visibility checker](https://ahrefs.com/free-ai-visibility)** queries ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot and AI Overviews with no signup, and calls its report "a limited preview."
- **HubSpot's [AI Search Grader](https://www.hubspot.com/ai-search-grader)** is a one-time check of what ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini say about you "based on their training data," so it reflects what the models learned rather than a live web search.
- **Free tiers and trials.** AthenaHQ's [Essential tier](https://athenahq.ai/plans) is free with 300 credits, at one AI response per credit. Profound's trial runs 50 prompts a day for 7 days. SE Visible offers a 10-day trial, and Semrush and LLMrefs offer 7 days.

A trial is most useful after a month of manual logging. You'll know which prompts matter, so you can judge whether the tool's numbers match yours.

## When to Pay: The Tracking Break-Even Test

We call this the Tracking Break-Even Test. It puts a price on your own time, because free tracking of brand mentions in AI search is only free if your hours are.

**Step 1. Price one logged answer.** Time your first full session and divide by the answers you logged. As an illustration, say running a prompt, reading the answer and filling the row takes 1.5 minutes, and your time is worth $40 an hour. One answer then costs 1.5 ÷ 60 × $40 = $1.00.

**Step 2. Count your monthly answers.** Prompts × engines × runs a week × 4.33 weeks.

**Step 3. Compare.** If answers a month × cost per answer is higher than a plan that covers the same prompts and engines, pay. If it's lower, stay free.

Here is the test for Plannora, a made-up project management tool. It logs 20 prompts in three engines once a week: 20 × 3 × 1 = 60 answers a week, and 60 × 4.33 = 260 answers a month. At $1.00 each, that's $260 of time a month, or 6.5 hours.

| Plan (price as listed 28 Sep 2026) | Break-even, answers a month | Covers Plannora's 20 prompts and 3 engines? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Otterly.AI Lite, $29 | 29 | No: 15 prompts, and AI Mode is an add-on |
| HubSpot AEO, $50 | 50 | Partly: 25 prompts on ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity |
| Peec AI Starter, $95 | 95 | Partly: 50 prompts on 3 models, with Perplexity as an add-on |
| Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit, $99 | 99 | Partly: 25 prompts; the plan lists "Google AI" rather than AI Mode by name |

Break-even is the plan price divided by $1.00 an answer. Plannora's 260 manual answers are well past every line, so paying is cheaper than its time. But the last column matters more than the price. No plan above matches Plannora's exact engine mix, so it either changes its mix or picks the plan that covers the engines its buyers use. Coverage first, price second.

### Signs you should stay free

- You track fewer than about 100 answers a month, so the hours are small.
- You want to read the answers yourself, to learn which sites engines lean on.
- You report to yourself, not to a client or a board.

### Signs it's time to pay

- You need more runs than a person can log. Steady numbers on brand mentions in AI search take repeat runs: the 2026 study ["Don't Measure Once"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07585) advises at least 7 runs per prompt per day for brand visibility.
- You track several countries or languages.
- You need months of history and exports for a monthly report.
- You run an agency and track several brands at once.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is there a free way to track brand mentions in AI search?

Yes. Run 15 to 20 buyer prompts by hand in two or three engines each week and log whether each answer names or links you. Add Bing's AI Performance report, Search Console's generative AI report and GA4's AI Assistant channel. All of it costs nothing but time.

### Does Google Search Console show brand mentions in AI Overviews?

No, it shows impressions of your URLs. Google's generative AI reports count how often your pages appeared in AI Overviews, AI Mode and Discover, by page, country, device and date. A mention of your name without your URL won't appear, so keep a prompt log alongside it.

### Can GA4 show which AI assistants mention my brand?

Not mentions, only visits. GA4's AI Assistant channel counts sessions from ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Copilot and Grok, and excludes Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode. Answers nobody clicks never reach GA4, so it shows only part of your AI visibility.

### How much does it cost to track brand mentions in AI search?

It can cost nothing if you log answers yourself. As listed on 28 September 2026, paid entry plans ran from $29 a month (Otterly.AI Lite) to $99 (Semrush, SE Visible and Chatbeat), while Profound starts with a free 7-day trial and then a custom contract.

### Are free AI visibility checkers accurate?

They're accurate snapshots, not trends. Each runs a small set of prompts once, and AI answers change from run to run. HubSpot's grader also reads training data rather than live search. Use a checker for a first look, then a weekly log or a tracker for anything you report.

### When should a small business pay for AI mention tracking?

Pay when your manual log passes roughly 100 answers a month, when you need several countries, or when you report to clients. Before that, a weekly spreadsheet and the free vendor reports cover most small businesses, and the time spent reading answers teaches you what engines rely on.
