Generative engine optimization (GEO) is the work of getting your brand named, and your pages cited, inside answers written by AI systems such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI Overviews. GEO tools split into six jobs: tracking what answers say, creating pages worth quoting, opening crawl access, adding structured data, publishing llms.txt, and earning mentions on other sites. Almost every job has a free option good enough to start with.

Which job to fund first depends on what's broken. Our [AEO audit guide](/blog/aeo-audit) scores 20 points across these same areas, so run it before you buy anything. This list then gives you a tool for each failing point, with every price as listed on the vendor's page on 29 September 2026.

Keep one fact in mind while you shop. Google's [AI optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) says "no third-party tool has access to our internal ranking or AI systems." GEO tools watch answers from the outside, fix your site, or help you publish. None of them can switch a citation on.

## Key Takeaways

- GEO means earning mentions and citations inside AI answers. The term comes from a 2023 research paper that reported gains of up to 40% in visibility.
- Sort GEO tools by job: tracking, content, technical access, structured data, llms.txt, and mentions and PR.
- Free first-party reports come first. Bing Webmaster Tools counts Copilot citations, and Search Console counts AI Overviews impressions.
- Paid entry points on 29 September 2026 ran from $9.99 a month (F5Bot Silver, for Reddit alerts) to $199 a month billed yearly (Brand24, for mention monitoring).
- Structured data and llms.txt rarely need paid tools. Google says neither is required for its AI features.
- Buy for the symptom you can see, and test a tool on your own pages before you commit.

## Generative Engine Optimization in One Paragraph

The phrase comes from ["GEO: Generative Engine Optimization"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735), a paper first posted in November 2023 by researchers from Princeton and other institutions. Its authors reported that changing how content is written "can boost visibility by up to 40% in generative engine responses." In practice, GEO keeps the SEO base (crawlable, indexed, useful pages) and aims at a different prize: a place inside the answer, not a blue link below it. For the full explainer, with GEO vs SEO vs AEO, read our [plain-English guide to generative engine optimization](/blog/what-is-generative-engine-optimization). The glossary entry on [answer engine optimization](/glossary/answer-engine-optimization) covers the narrower term.

### 1. Tracking: are AI answers naming you?

Tracking tools run buyer prompts in AI engines and record who gets named and cited. Start with the two reports the engines' owners give you. Bing's [AI Performance report](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/February-2026/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview) counts your citations across Copilot and Bing's AI summaries, with the grounding queries behind them. Search Console's [generative AI report](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16984139) counts impressions in AI Overviews and AI Mode.

For other engines, two free checks give a quick read. Semrush's [AI visibility checker](https://www.semrush.com/free-tools/ai-search-visibility-checker/) covers ChatGPT, Gemini, AI Overviews and AI Mode, three times a day with no account. HubSpot's [AI Search Grader](https://www.hubspot.com/ai-search-grader) is free and asks GPT-5.4 mini, Perplexity and Gemini how they describe your brand "based on its training data," so it reflects what models learned rather than a live search.

Paid trackers run the same prompts every day. On 29 September 2026, [Otterly.AI](https://otterly.ai/pricing) Lite listed at $29 a month for 15 prompts in four engines, [Peec AI](https://peec.ai/pricing) Starter at $95 for 50 prompts on three models, and [SE Visible](https://visible.seranking.com/) Basic at $99 for 200 prompts in five engines, with a 10-day trial. Nine trackers are compared in depth in our [ChatGPT rank tracker guide](/blog/chatgpt-rank-tracker), and our guide to [tracking brand mentions for free](/blog/track-brand-mentions-in-ai-search-free-and-paid) covers the manual route.

### 2. Content: pages worth quoting

Content tools come in two kinds. Graders score a draft against pages that already rank. Content engines research and write the pages themselves.

- **Graders:** [Surfer](https://surferseo.com/pricing/) Discovery is $49 a month billed yearly, and [Clearscope](https://www.clearscope.io/pricing) Essentials is $129 a month with a 14-day trial.
- **Content engines:** [Frase](https://www.frase.io/pricing) Starter is $49 a month, or $39 billed yearly, for 10 articles, 50 audit pages and 50 tracked AI prompts. Rankbox is $49.50 a month for 30 articles on one site.

### 3. Technical and crawl access

If AI bots are blocked from a page, nothing else on this list matters. These GEO tools read robots.txt, logs and firewall settings.

- **Free:** Cloudflare says [AI Crawl Control](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-crawl-control/) is "available on all plans" and shows which AI services reach your content. Rankbox's [robots.txt tester](/tools/robots-txt-tester), [AI crawler log analyzer](/tools/ai-crawler-log-analyzer) and [AI search readiness check](/tools/ai-search-readiness-check) cover rules, log hits and pages that need JavaScript.
- **Crawlers:** the [Screaming Frog SEO Spider](https://www.screamingfrog.co.uk/seo-spider/pricing/) is free up to 500 URLs, and a licence costs $279 a year.
- **Bot analytics:** [Known Agents](https://knownagents.com/pricing) has a free plan with 100,000 events a month, and Startup is $29 a month for 1 million.

For the bots to check, see [The AI Crawler Directory](/blog/ai-crawler-directory).

### 4. Structured data

Structured data tells machines who you are and what each page holds. Google says there's ["no special schema.org structured data"](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) you need for its AI features, and Bing's [guidelines](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/webmaster-guidelines-30fba23a) say markup "must accurately reflect visible content." So the work is accuracy, and free tools handle it.

- Rankbox's [schema generator](/tools/schema-generator) writes JSON-LD for ten types, including Organization and Person.
- The [Schema Markup Validator](https://validator.schema.org/), run by schema.org, checks any type.
- Google's [Rich Results Test](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7445569) checks only the types that earn rich results in Google, so use the validator for everything else.

Our guide to [building a knowledge graph for AI](/blog/knowledge-graph-for-ai) shows how to connect the pieces.

### 5. llms.txt

An llms.txt file is a Markdown list of your key pages for AI agents, proposed at [llmstxt.org](https://llmstxt.org/). Rankbox's [llms.txt generator](/tools/llms-txt-generator) writes one in the spec's format. On WordPress, Yoast says its llms.txt feature is ["free for everyone"](https://yoast.com/features/llms-txt/) and generated and refreshed automatically.

Set expectations first. Google says such files "will neither harm nor help" your visibility in its Search. Our post on [whether llms.txt helps your SEO](/blog/will-llms-txt-help-your-seo) covers who does read it.

### 6. Mentions and PR

AI answers lean on what other sites say about you. In [Ahrefs' December 2025 study](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-brand-visibility-correlations/) of 75,000 brands, YouTube mentions correlated with AI visibility at about 0.737, and branded web mentions at 0.664 for ChatGPT. Those are correlations, not causes, but they explain why this job belongs on a GEO tools list.

- **Free:** [F5Bot](https://f5bot.com/) alerts you when your keywords appear on Reddit, Hacker News and Lobsters. Its Silver plan is $9.99 a month. [Qwoted](https://www.qwoted.com/pricing/) Basic is free with two pitches a month to reporters.
- **Paid:** Qwoted Pro is $149 a month for 35 pitches. [Brand24](https://brand24.com/prices/) starts at $199 a month billed yearly, with a 14-day trial.
- **Rolling out:** Rankbox's [Reddit Presence](/features/reddit-presence) finds relevant threads and drafts replies for you to post yourself.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are GEO tools?

GEO tools are software that help your brand get named and cited in AI answers from engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews. They cover six jobs: tracking answers, creating quotable pages, checking crawl access, adding structured data, publishing llms.txt and earning mentions on other sites.

### Are there free GEO tools?

Yes. Bing Webmaster Tools and Search Console report AI citations and impressions for free. Semrush's checker gives three free AI visibility checks a day. Cloudflare's AI Crawl Control is on all plans, and Rankbox's robots.txt tester, log analyzer, schema generator and llms.txt generator are free.

### What is the best GEO tool?

There isn't one best GEO tool, because each does a different job. Trackers measure answers, and content engines write pages, though a few products such as Frase sell both. Pick the job your audit scores lowest on, then compare tools inside that job on your own prompts.

### Do I need GEO tools to do generative engine optimization?

No. You can run GEO with free reports, a spreadsheet of buyer prompts and your own writing. Paid GEO tools save time once your prompt list, page count or pitch volume outgrows manual work. Start free, and pay for the job that eats the most hours.

### How are GEO tools different from SEO tools?

SEO tools report rankings and clicks on a results page. GEO tools report whether AI answers name or cite you, check access for AI crawlers, and help create pages engines quote. Many vendors now sell both, so check which job a plan actually covers.

### Is llms.txt a GEO tool?

An llms.txt file is one small part of GEO, not a tool in itself. It lists your key pages for AI agents that choose to read it. Google says its Search ignores the file, so treat it as optional housekeeping after crawl access and content.
