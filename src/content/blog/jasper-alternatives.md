## Jasper in 2026: One Seat, Then a Sales Call

Jasper now calls itself "the marketing agents platform, built to help enterprises orchestrate AI agents." Its [pricing page](https://www.jasper.ai/pricing) shows two plans, and the Creator plan that older reviews quote is gone.

![Jasper's pricing page with Monthly selected: Pro at $69 a month per seat with a Start Free 7-Day Trial button, and Business at custom pricing with a Contact Sales button](/images/blog/jasper-pricing-2026-09-23.webp "Jasper's pricing page on 23 September 2026, monthly view.")

**Pro** is $69 a month, or $59 billed yearly. The help center is blunt: "Our Pro plan is limited to 1 seat." It buys the Canvas workspace, agents for core marketing workflows, 2 Brand Voices, 5 Knowledge assets, 3 Audiences, and a few AI-search writing agents such as a Query Planner. The 7-day trial takes a card, with no charge if you cancel during it, and refund requests must come within 7 days of a charge.

**Business** is custom-priced, and its plans "begin with a 12 month commitment." That's where you'll find extra seats, Grid for bulk content, Studio for building agents, the API and MCP server, the Research and Optimization agents, and GEO Hub. Several run on credits: a GEO Hub query or page run costs 10 credits, and Jasper doesn't publish what a credit costs in dollars.

GEO Hub launched on 16 June 2026. It tracks brand presence, citation rate, share of voice, and sentiment across ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity. At least two ranking lists of Jasper alternatives still say Jasper has no [AI visibility](/glossary/ai-visibility) tracking. It does, just not on Pro.

Publishing is mostly up to you. Jasper downloads Word, PDF, text, and Markdown files and exports to Google Drive, SharePoint, and Box. Its only CMS app is for Webflow, and WordPress runs through Zapier or Make. Drafting in Jasper and polishing in Surfer is over too, since Surfer's page now reads "Jasper integration is no longer available."

eesel AI's review names the main complaint: "a single-seat Pro plan that forces teams into a custom contract." It also cites "output that reads generic without heavy editing," a risk with any AI writer, and notes that "plain old ChatGPT quietly replaces Jasper for a surprising number of teams."

Billed as "the content operating system for AI search," it studies what ranks, turns that into a brief, writes a draft, and grades it for SEO, GEO, and [E-E-A-T](/glossary/e-e-a-t), Google's shorthand for experience, expertise, authoritativeness, and trustworthiness. Seats are where it beats Jasper Pro. [Starter](https://www.frase.io/pricing) is $49 a month, or $39 yearly, for one seat, one site, 10 articles, and 50 tracked prompts in ChatGPT and Google AI. Starter's articles cost $4.90 each on monthly billing, and Frase states no target length. Its terms say "All fees paid are final, and we do not provide any refunds," so use the 7-day trial, which doesn't ask for a card. See our [Frase alternatives](/blog/frase-alternatives).

### 3. Surfer: the optimization half of the old pairing

If you wrote in Jasper and scored in Surfer, only the Surfer half is still on offer. It turned up on 7 of the 13 lists of Jasper alternatives we read.

Surfer, styled "Positive Surfer" since Positive Group bought it in October 2025, is an editor. It shows the terms and structure top pages share and grades your draft from 0 to 100, blending an SEO score with an AI Search score. Surfer AI can draft inside the editor, but a person still shapes each piece. Scheduled publishing isn't its job.

Its seat ladder is kinder to teams than Jasper's:

- **Discovery**, in a "Start smaller" section lower on the [pricing page](https://surferseo.com/pricing/): $59 a month, or $49 yearly, for one seat and 10 Documents a month. It has no AI tracker, keyword research, or integrations.
- **Standard:** $119, or $99 yearly, for three seats, 30 Documents a month, WordPress and Google Docs integrations, and weekly tracking of 25 ChatGPT prompts.
- **Pro:** $219, or $182 yearly, for five seats and daily tracking across five AI models.

A Document is one editor you open. The 7-day trial unlocks Pro, takes a card, and becomes a paid plan unless you cancel. Rivals are in our [Surfer SEO alternatives](/blog/surfer-seo-alternatives).

## Jasper Alternatives for Ads, Email, and Social Copy

Channel copy is where Jasper alternatives get cheapest, from performance-scored ads down to a $9 plan for one person.

### 4. Anyword: copy scored before it goes live

Anyword is for performance marketers. Before an ad, subject line, or headline ships, it predicts how each variant will do from A/B-test data, so you run the likelier winner. It claims 82% accuracy at picking the better of two variants (Anyword's own figure). Around that sit unlimited copy, brand voice rules, 100+ templates, CSV bulk generation, 30+ languages, and a Chrome extension.

**For a team.** [Starter](https://www.anyword.com/pricing) is $49 a month, or $39 yearly, for one seat, one brand voice, and 50 predictions a month (100 on yearly billing). Data-Driven is $99, or $79 yearly, for three seats. More seats are $59 a month each, up to 10, so five people pay $217. Business is custom, and it's the plan that learns from your channel data and connects to Meta Ads, Google Ads, HubSpot, and Marketo.

**Where it stops.** Long-form SEO isn't the focus. Its CMS integrations are Sitecore and Drupal, and it doesn't track AI search. The 7-day trial caps at 2,500 words, and cancelled subscriptions aren't refunded. It showed 4.8 out of 5 on [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/anyword.com) from 5,207 reviews and 4.8 on Capterra from 391, both seen on 23 September 2026.

### 5. Copy.ai: five chat seats for $29

Copy.ai's Chat plan is the cheapest way on this list to seat a team: $29 a month, or $24 yearly, for five seats. That's $5.80 a seat by our arithmetic, with unlimited chat words and models from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Gemini. Brand Voice learns your tone from past content, a workspace can hold any number of voices, and Infobase keeps your style guide, positioning, and personas on hand.

It isn't the Copy.ai that 12 of the 13 lists of Jasper alternatives describe, though. Fullcast, a revenue-operations company, [announced it had acquired Copy.ai](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/fullcast-announces-the-acquisition-of-copyai-302584121.html) in October 2025, and the homepage now reads "The First AI-Native GTM Platform." Templates became chat with a prompt library, and the free plan was sunset.

Before you move a team:

- Chat has no workflow credits. [Workflows](https://www.copy.ai/prices), which chain research, writing, and translation, start on Growth at $1,000 a month, billed yearly as $12,000.
- No specific CMS connector is named, AI search tracking isn't offered, and the public changelog stops in June 2025.
- [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/copy.ai) showed 1.8 out of 5 from 196 reviews on 23 September 2026, with some recent reviews citing login and support problems. Capterra showed 4.4 from 67 reviews dated 2023 to 2025.
- Refunds come as a 5-day money-back guarantee.

### 6. Rytr: the $9 plan for one person

Rytr is for the solo marketer who used Jasper's old templates for quick copy. There's no team plan. It has 40+ use cases, from emails and captions to meta titles and calls to action, plus 20+ preset tones and Tone Match, which writes in your voice.

A [free plan](https://rytr.me/pricing) covers 10,000 characters a month. Unlimited is $9 a month, or $7.50 yearly, with one Tone Match and 50 plagiarism checks. Premium is $29, or $24.16 yearly, and adds more Tone Matches, 35+ languages, and custom use cases. Yearly plans are a 12-month commitment.

Don't expect researched long-form articles, SEO data, or CMS publishing. In December 2024 the FTC approved a consent order that bars Rytr from selling any service promoted as generating consumer reviews or testimonials. It's a settlement, not a finding of guilt. Rytr showed 4.4 out of 5 from 2,449 Trustpilot reviews on 23 September 2026.

## For Governed Teams and Everyday Writing

Two opposite answers to "we need AI for the whole team." One adds more control than Jasper Pro. The other trades control for cheaper seats.

### 7. WRITER: the closest thing to Jasper Business

If brand and compliance controls are why you bought Jasper, WRITER is the closest match to its Business plan. It calls itself "The enterprise AI platform for agentic work," under the line "Not a tool you prompt. An agent you delegate to." Teams get an agent for chat and automation, reusable workflows called Playbooks, a Knowledge Graph of company context, and voice profiles that apply brand rules to the output.

Enterprise adds departmental voice profiles, SSO, SCIM, and audit logs. WRITER describes a "zero data retention approach" and shares SOC 2 Type II and HIPAA reports with Enterprise customers on request. Its [connectors](https://writer.com/product/connectors/) push content into Contentful, Adobe Experience Manager, and Webflow. No WordPress connector is listed.

The [Starter plan](https://writer.com/plans/) showed $39 a user a month, or $29 yearly, until late 2025. Then the price came off the page. Starter allows 5 users, 5 Playbooks, one team voice profile, and 3 active connectors, with a 14-day trial that doesn't ask for a card. AI search tracking isn't its focus, and refunds are at WRITER's discretion. It suits large marketing teams in regulated industries best.

### 8. ChatGPT Business and Claude Team: $25 seats for everyday writing

Plenty of teams swap Jasper for a general assistant. In September 2026 the team plans of ChatGPT and Claude cost the same: $25 a seat a month, or $20 yearly, with a two-seat minimum. Premium seats with five times the usage are $125, or $100 yearly, and you can mix seat types. Five standard seats cost $125 a month, while Jasper Pro covers one person for $69.

- **[ChatGPT Business](https://openai.com/business/chatgpt-pricing/)**, renamed from ChatGPT Team in August 2025, includes custom GPTs, Projects, Apps, Company Knowledge, ChatGPT Agent, and Deep Research.
- **[Claude Team](https://claude.com/pricing)**, sold "for teams of 2 to 150," includes shared Projects, Skills you can deploy across the organization, connectors, web research, file creation, and org-wide instructions.

Neither trains on your business content by default, and neither is a marketing product. With no brand-voice module, style-guide check, content calendar, or approval flow, house style lives in the Projects, GPTs, or Skills you set up. There's no SEO data or CMS publishing, every seat has usage limits, and Claude lists no trial for Team. If you want article research in the same window, Rankbox's research tools connect to both through MCP.

## Keep Jasper If One of These Is True

Jasper covers more ground than any one pick above. Before you switch to any of these Jasper alternatives, check these four.

1. **You write for every channel.** Jasper's agents cover ads, email, social, web copy, press releases, and product descriptions, plus an image suite. Most picks here cover part of that range, and Rankbox covers only articles.
2. **Brand rules span a whole marketing org.** Jasper IQ holds Brand Voice, Knowledge, and Audiences, and Business adds a Style Guide and Visual Guidelines. Jasper lists SSO, SCIM, and SOC 2 Type II.
3. **You want AI tracking and fixes on one contract.** On Business, GEO Hub watches four AI engines and the GEO Agent drafts fixes. Rankbox has no live citation monitoring yet.
4. **You produce in bulk or in many languages.** Grid, on Business, runs generation at scale, such as thousands of product descriptions, and the Translation Agent covers 27 languages.

If one of those decides it, Jasper's price buys something real, and nonprofits get 20% off. You can also keep Jasper for channel copy and give the blog to another tool; our page for [marketers](/use-cases/marketers) shows how Rankbox fits that split.

## Leaving Jasper Without Losing Work

Whichever Jasper alternatives you pick, the pages you've published live on your own site and stay put. Then work through this list.

1. **Save your brand context.** Put Brand Voice notes, style rules, audiences, and knowledge documents in a file you own.
2. **Export your drafts.** Jasper downloads Word, PDF, text, or Markdown files, and exports to Google Drive, SharePoint, or Box once an admin allows it.
3. **Pause rather than cancel.** A monthly Pro plan can pause for 30 days while you trial a replacement. A yearly plan is a 12-month commitment once its 7-day refund window closes.
4. **Keep ranking URLs.** If a page has to move, 301-redirect it. Our [redirect generator](/tools/redirect-generator) writes the rules.
5. **Test on real work.** Time a brief you'd have given Jasper all the way to a publishable page, on a topic you haven't covered, so two articles don't fight over one query ([keyword cannibalization](/glossary/keyword-cannibalization)).

If the blog is what you'd hand off, [Rankbox's 7-day trial](/pricing) writes up to 7 articles for your site, enough to set beside what Jasper gave you.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best Jasper alternative?

It depends on the job, because Jasper alternatives tend to do one of Jasper's jobs well rather than all of them. Rankbox fits SEO articles delivered on a schedule, at $49.50 a month for 30 articles. Anyword fits ads and email, WRITER fits enterprise governance, and ChatGPT Business or Claude Team fits cheap everyday seats at $25 each.

### Is there a free or cheaper alternative to Jasper?

Rytr has a free plan with 10,000 characters a month, and its Unlimited plan is $9 a month. Copy.ai's Chat plan covers five seats for $29 a month, against $69 for one seat on Jasper Pro. Most other Jasper alternatives offer trials rather than free plans, including Rankbox, WRITER, Anyword, Writesonic, Frase, and Surfer.

### Can I use ChatGPT instead of Jasper?

For everyday drafting, yes, and many teams do. ChatGPT Business costs $25 a seat a month with a two-seat minimum and includes Projects, custom GPTs, and Deep Research. You give up marketing structure, such as brand-voice rules, style-guide checks, and channel agents, and rebuild it with your own instructions.

### Which Jasper alternatives publish straight to WordPress?

Frase, Writesonic, and Surfer's Standard plan connect to WordPress. Rankbox delivers through its publishing API today, with a WordPress plugin in development. WRITER and Anyword list no WordPress connector, and Jasper reaches WordPress through Zapier or Make.

### Is Jasper worth it in 2026?

It can be, for marketing teams writing across many channels under strict brand rules, especially on Business with GEO Hub and Grid. If your team mostly needs blog posts, or you work alone on a tight budget, several Jasper alternatives above cost less for that narrower job.
