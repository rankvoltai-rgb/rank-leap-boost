Most of SEO carries straight over. Answer engine optimization (AEO) keeps the crawling, indexing, quality and authority work of classic search, and changes three things: the target (being the answer rather than a ranked link), the unit you shape (a passage and an entity rather than a page and a keyword) and the scoreboard (share of answers rather than position). That's the short version of answer engine optimization vs SEO.

Google goes further and says nothing extra is needed for its own AI features. Its [documentation on AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features), last updated on 10 December 2025, says: "There are no additional requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, nor other special optimizations necessary." That statement covers Google only. ChatGPT, Perplexity and other answer engines publish far less about how they choose sources.

This post is for SEO teams weighing answer engine optimization vs SEO in their own plans. It covers what stays, what changes, what the search engines have said on the record, and a checklist for the switch. For how AEO compares with its close cousin GEO, see our [full guide to AEO vs. GEO](/blog/aeo-vs-geo).

## Key Takeaways

- In answer engine optimization vs SEO, the foundation is shared: a page must be crawled, indexed and eligible for a snippet before Google's AI features can use it.
- Google says its AI features need "no additional requirements" beyond SEO, and it lists llms.txt files, content chunking and special schema as things to ignore.
- What changes is the target and the unit: a passage that answers a question on its own, and a brand whose facts are clear and consistent across the web.
- Measurement changes most. Clicks fall when an AI summary appears: Pew found 8% of visits with a summary led to a result click, against 15% without one.
- An SEO team can switch in about a quarter with twelve changes to audits, briefs, reporting and outreach. Most are edits to work it already does.

## What Stays the Same in Answer Engine Optimization vs SEO

### Crawling and indexing

A page an engine can't fetch can't become an answer. Google says a page must be "indexed and eligible to be shown in Google Search with a snippet" to show as a supporting link in AI Overviews or AI Mode. OpenAI's [help page on ChatGPT search](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9237897-chatgpt-search) says that to be eligible, a site must "allow OAI-Searchbot to crawl the site." Bing's [webmaster guidelines](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/webmaster-guidelines-30fba23a) say its Copilot experiences "rely on the same core crawling, indexing, and ranking foundation as traditional search."

So your technical SEO checks stay. You just add a few user agents to them.

### Content quality

Google's [May 2025 post on AI search](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2025/05/succeeding-in-ai-search) says "the underpinnings of what Google has long advised carries across to these new experiences." Its 2026 guide adds that content people find "unique, compelling, and useful" will likely matter more over time than any other tip it gives. Helpful, original pages were the goal before AI answers, and they still are.

### Links and authority

Authority still counts, but the kind shifts. Bing still lists link buying and link schemes as abuse. For AI answers, being talked about seems to matter more than link counts. Ahrefs' [December 2025 study of 75,000 brands](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-brand-visibility-correlations/) found brand mentions, and YouTube mentions most of all, correlated with AI visibility more strongly than link and content volume, which showed little impact. That's a correlation, not proof of cause.

### Structured data

Markup rules don't change. Google says structured data isn't required for its AI features and that there's no special schema for them, but markup should still match the visible page. One SEO habit does need updating: FAQ rich results stopped showing in Google Search in May 2026, per Google's [documentation changelog](https://developers.google.com/search/updates).

## What Changes in Answer Engine Optimization vs SEO

The table sets answer engine optimization vs SEO side by side. The left column is classic SEO; the right is what AEO adds or replaces.

|                    | Classic SEO                      | Answer engine optimization                                        |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Target             | A ranked link on a results page  | The passage an engine uses as the answer                          |
| Unit you shape     | A page, matched to a keyword     | A section that answers one question, plus your brand as an entity |
| Query you plan for | What the person typed            | The question, and the follow-up searches the engine runs          |
| How a page opens   | Often context first              | The answer first, then the reasoning                              |
| Off-site work      | Links to your pages              | Mentions in the sources engines read, links included              |
| Main metric        | Position, impressions and clicks | Share of tracked questions where you supply the answer            |
| Click expectation  | A click is the goal              | Many answers end without a click, so a mention is a result        |

### Answer-first passages

An answer engine lifts a section, not a whole page. In Kevin Indig's analysis of 18,012 verified ChatGPT citations, [reported by Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/chatgpt-citations-content-study-469483), 44.2% came from the first 30% of the page, and 78.4% of citations tied to questions came from headings. So the habit to build is a question heading with a first sentence that answers it on its own.

There's a tension here worth knowing. Google's guide says "you don't need to write in a specific way just for generative AI search." Both can be true. Google can find the answer in a messy page; a page that states it up front is easier for every engine, and for readers. Our guide to [optimizing content for AI search](/blog/optimize-content-for-ai-search) shows the edits passage by passage.

### Entity clarity

Classic SEO asks whether a page matches a keyword. AEO also asks whether the engine knows who you are. That means a plain definition of what you sell, the same facts (name, price, location, founding year) on your site and your public profiles, and a page that states those facts in one place. Our post on [entity authority in SEO](/blog/what-is-entity-authority-in-seo) explains the idea in more depth.

### Measurement

Measurement is where answer engine optimization vs SEO differ most. Rank and clicks no longer tell the whole story. In [Pew Research Center's study](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/) of 68,879 Google searches, people clicked a result in 8% of visits that showed an AI summary, against 15% when there was none. Google now reports AI Overviews and AI Mode impressions in Search Console's [generative AI performance report](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/gen-ai-performance-reports), launched on 3 June 2026. For engines that report nothing, you run your own question panel. Our guide to [measuring GEO](/blog/how-to-measure-geo) covers sample sizes and a control test.

## What Search Engines Have Said, With Dates

On answer engine optimization vs SEO, search engines rarely comment directly, so their statements are worth reading closely. Here they are in date order.

1. **21 May 2025, Google.** John Mueller wrote that "meeting the technical requirements covers you for search generally, including AI formats."
2. **Reported 31 October 2025, Google.** Robby Stein, VP of Product for Google Search, said of SEO and AI search optimization: "There's a lot of overlap," adding that people ask AI more complicated questions ([Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/seo-ai-search-optimization-overlap-robby-stein-464028)).
3. **19 November 2025, Google and Bing.** Quoted by Business Insider and [summarized by Search Engine Roundtable](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-microsoft-perplexity-geo-rush-40457.html), Danny Sullivan warned that work done only "to please the system may not carry through to the long term." Bing's Krishna Madhavan told site owners to "think beyond keywords to user intent, question‑answer structure, and machine‑readable cues."
4. **10 December 2025, Google.** The AI features documentation says there are "no additional requirements" for AI Overviews or AI Mode.
5. **15 May 2026, Google.** Google's [guide to generative AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) says AEO and GEO work is, from its view, "still SEO," and tells site owners to prioritize "effective SEO strategies over 'AEO/GEO hacks.'"

One analyst view balances these. Forrester's Nikhil Lai [wrote in November 2025](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/seos-hype-fueled-move-to-the-center-of-the-marketing-mix/) that "AEO is significantly, but not fundamentally, different from SEO." That matches the answer engine optimization vs SEO split in this post: the base is shared, but the target, unit and scoreboard change.

Keep in mind who is speaking. Google and Bing describe their own systems. OpenAI says only that ChatGPT "ranks search results using multiple factors" and that "placement is not guaranteed." For engines like that, the evidence comes from outside studies, not official rules.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference in answer engine optimization vs SEO?

SEO aims for a ranked link on a results page. Answer engine optimization aims for your passage to be the answer an engine shows or speaks. The foundation is the same: crawling, indexing, quality and authority. AEO changes the target, the unit you shape and how you measure success.

### Does AEO replace SEO?

No. Answer engines retrieve pages from search indexes, so a page that isn't crawled and indexed can't be the answer. Google says its AI features need no special work beyond SEO. AEO adds goals on top of SEO rather than replacing it.

### Does Google require special optimization for AI Overviews?

No. Google says there are "no additional requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, nor other special optimizations necessary." A page must be indexed and eligible for a snippet. Google also says you can ignore llms.txt files, content chunking and special schema for its AI features.

### Is answer engine optimization vs SEO a different skill set?

Mostly the same skills, aimed differently. SEO teams already do the technical, content and outreach work AEO needs. The new parts are question research, answer-first writing, keeping company facts consistent across the web, and measuring share of answers instead of rank alone.

### How do you measure AEO compared with SEO?

SEO uses rank, impressions and clicks. AEO adds the share of tracked questions where your page supplies the answer, measured with a fixed question panel run in each engine. Google's Search Console now also reports impressions in AI Overviews and AI Mode.
