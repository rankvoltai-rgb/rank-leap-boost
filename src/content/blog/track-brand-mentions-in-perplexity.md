To track brand mentions in Perplexity for free, run a fixed set of 20 to 30 buyer prompts each week in a clean session, and log whether each answer names you, where, and which sources it cites. To pay instead, pick a tracker whose entry plan lists Perplexity. As listed on 1 October 2026, Otterly.AI starts at $29 a month, and HubSpot AEO and Ahrefs' prompt packages start at $50.

Perplexity is the easiest engine to log by hand, because every answer shows its sources and you can highlight a sentence to see where it came from. The hard part is consistency: the same session, the same prompts and enough runs to see a trend. For why names and links move separately, read our [guide to getting cited by Perplexity AI](/blog/get-cited-by-perplexity). This post is the tracking method, and it covers Perplexity only. For tools that span several engines, see [how to track brand mentions in AI search for free](/blog/track-brand-mentions-in-ai-search-free-and-paid).

## Key Takeaways

- A free Perplexity mention log needs four things: a fixed prompt set, a clean session, the same fields for every answer, and a month of data before you read a trend.
- Use incognito mode or a signed-out window. Perplexity says memory and past searches are always off in incognito, and signed-out threads are anonymous.
- Perplexity's own features help: History, Spaces for grouping audit threads, Tasks for scheduling up to ten prompts, and export to Markdown.
- Perplexity offers site owners no brand or citation report. Its help center describes none as of October 2026.
- Brand names repeat more than sources. In a 2026 study, Perplexity's named brands overlapped 0.492 between runs, its sources 0.282.
- Paid trackers that list Perplexity start at $29 a month (Otterly.AI Lite, 15 prompts daily) as listed on 1 October 2026.

## The Free Method: A Weekly Perplexity Mention Log

You can track brand mentions in Perplexity with a spreadsheet and an hour a week. The method has three steps.

### Step 1: Fix the prompt set

Write 20 to 30 prompts and keep them for at least a quarter. Mix four kinds:

1. **Category prompts** with no brand in them: "best invoicing software for agencies."
2. **Comparisons:** "Tallyfold vs Brindlework for a 10-person studio."
3. **Alternatives:** "alternatives to Kestrelyn."
4. **Questions about you:** "Tallyfold pricing" or "is Tallyfold legit?"

Add a second phrasing for your five most important prompts, since buyers word the same need in different ways. Our free [AI Visibility Prompt Kit](/tools/ai-visibility-prompt-generator) drafts 30 prompts across the buying journey, with a scorecard.

### Step 2: Track brand mentions in Perplexity from a clean session

Perplexity's answers can change with who is asking. Its help center documents four session types, and they behave differently:

| Session                           | What Perplexity documents                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Use it for                                           |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Signed out                        | Threads are "anonymous" and stay visible for [14 days](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/10354769-what-is-a-thread)                                                                                                                              | Quick checks with nothing personal attached          |
| Incognito, signed in              | "Memory and previous searches are always off" ([memory article](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/10968016-memory)); threads [expire within 24 hours](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/12639758-incognito-mode-troubleshooting) | Your weekly panel; copy the answers out the same day |
| Signed in, memory and history off | Both can be [toggled off in Personalize](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/10968016-memory); threads stay in History                                                                                                                             | Scheduled Tasks (below)                              |
| Signed in, default                | Memory and past searches may shape answers, and show up in the sources                                                                                                                                                                                           | Not for tracking                                     |

Two more settings matter. Location is opt-in: account settings offer ["Share location"](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/10352993-account-settings) for location-aware answers, so leave it off or keep it the same. And keep the search mode and model fixed. A thread's Source button tells you "which Model, Search mode and Source mode was applied," so record it.

### Step 3: Log the same fields every time

| Field                   | How to fill it                                 | Where to find it in Perplexity     |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Date, prompt ID, run    | 2026-10-05, cat-03, 2                          | Your sheet                         |
| Mode and model          | "Pro Search, Best"                             | The Source button on the answer    |
| Named                   | Yes or no                                      | The answer text                    |
| Position                | 1 if you're the first brand named, 2 if second | The answer text                    |
| Cited                   | Yes if any of your URLs is a source            | The source list                    |
| Cited domains           | Every domain in the source list                | Three-dots menu, then View Sources |
| Source behind your name | The page the naming sentence cites             | Highlight the sentence             |
| Rivals named            | Brindlework, Kestrelyn                         | The answer text                    |
| Accuracy                | Correct, outdated or wrong                     | Your own facts                     |

The "source behind your name" column is the one most logs skip, and it's the most useful. It tells you which page speaks for you. If you're named but not cited, it's usually a list or a thread, as our post on [brand mentions in Perplexity](/blog/brand-mentions-in-perplexity) explains.

Keep the raw answers too. The [Export button](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/10354769-what-is-a-thread) saves an answer as PDF, Markdown or DOCX, so you can check a surprising row later.

## What Perplexity's Own Features Can Do for Tracking

Note what Perplexity doesn't offer, too. Its help center and docs describe no dashboard or report that shows site owners how often they're named or cited. Anything you track, you collect yourself or through a third-party tool.

### The Ten-Task Setup

If you pay for Perplexity Pro or Max, Tasks can help you track brand mentions in Perplexity by running your most important prompts for you. We call this the Ten-Task Setup, after the plan limit:

1. **Pick your ten most important prompts** from the full set. Favor category and comparison prompts.
2. **Turn off memory and search history** in Personalize, so past questions don't shape the runs.
3. **Create a Space** named for the log, with no custom instructions.
4. **Add one Space task per prompt.** Perplexity lets you set "a schedule, notification settings, search mode, model preference, and sources." Choose Weekly, the same mode and model for all ten, and Web as the source.
5. **Each week, open the new threads** and log the rows. Each run is saved as a thread in History.
6. **Once a month, run the same ten prompts in incognito** and compare. If the Task answers differ a lot, trust the incognito runs.

Ten tasks a week give you about 40 answers a month. That's enough to spot a big shift on your key prompts, not enough for a precise rate, so keep the manual panel for the rest. Perplexity doesn't say whether scheduled runs use location or other account context, which is why step 6 exists.

## How to Read the Log

When you track brand mentions in Perplexity, weekly numbers jump around. Pool four weeks before you read a rate.

The 2026 paper ["Don't Measure Once"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07585) explains why. It repeated German-language prompts on four engines within 24 hours. Perplexity's cited sources overlapped by a Jaccard score of 0.282, where 1 means identical. The brands it named overlapped by 0.492. Your mention rate will be steadier than your list of sources, but neither is steady week to week. The authors recommend reading brand visibility over a rolling two to four weeks.

The sample size sets how much change is real. Say you run 20 prompts twice a week: 40 answers a week, 160 a month. At a mention rate near 30%, one week's margin of error is about ±14 points. Four pooled weeks bring it to about ±7. Our [guide to measuring GEO](/blog/how-to-measure-geo) has the table and a control test for proving your work caused a change.

Report three numbers each month:

- **Mention rate:** answers that name you ÷ all answers.
- **Citation rate:** answers that cite one of your URLs ÷ all answers.
- **Top domains behind your name:** the pages that put your name in answers most often.

Here is an illustrative month for Tallyfold, a fictional invoicing app for agencies. It logged 160 answers. It was named in 56 (35%) and cited in 40 (25%). Of the 56 naming sentences, 21 cited a review site's roundup, 14 cited Tallyfold's own pages, 12 cited Reddit threads and 9 cited other pages (21 + 14 + 12 + 9 = 56). The roundup speaks for Tallyfold more often than Tallyfold does, so its next job is a comparison page of its own.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I track brand mentions in Perplexity for free?

Run 20 to 30 buyer prompts each week in Perplexity's incognito mode or a signed-out window. Log whether each answer names you, whether it cites your site, which domains it cites, and which page sits behind your name. Pool four weeks before reading a rate.

### Does Perplexity show how often my brand is mentioned?

No. As of October 2026, Perplexity's help center and docs describe no brand or citation report for site owners. You can see sources on any single answer, and highlight a sentence to see where it came from, but counting across answers is up to you or a tracker.

### Can I use Perplexity Tasks to track brand mentions?

Yes, for a few key prompts. Pro and Max allow ten Tasks, each run on a schedule and saved as a thread. Turn off memory and search history first, keep the mode and model fixed, and compare with an incognito run monthly.

### Should I track brand mentions in Perplexity signed in or signed out?

Signed out or in incognito. Perplexity says memory and past searches are always off in incognito, and signed-out threads are anonymous. A normal signed-in session can use your memory and history, which skews the result.

### Which tools track brand mentions in Perplexity?

As listed on 1 October 2026, entry plans that include Perplexity include Otterly.AI Lite at $29 a month, HubSpot AEO at $50, Ahrefs Brand Radar prompt packages from $50, Semrush's AI Visibility Toolkit at $99 per domain billed yearly, Surfer at $95, SE Visible at $99 and AthenaHQ's free tier.

### How many answers do I need to track brand mentions in Perplexity reliably?

About 160 a month gives a margin of roughly ±7 points on a 30% mention rate. That's 20 prompts run twice a week. Fewer answers still help you spot big changes, but a few points of movement will be noise.
