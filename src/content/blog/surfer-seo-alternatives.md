The best Surfer SEO alternatives depend on one question: does anyone on your team still want to write in an editor? If they do, Clearscope is the closest like-for-like swap and includes every user, NeuronWriter gives similar term guidance from $23 a month, and PageOptimizer Pro sets the most exact targets. If nobody does, Rankbox writes and checks 30 cited articles a month for $49.50 and sends them to your site, with no editor involved.

Surfer's editor is still very good. What changed in 2026 is the deal around it: new plans, older subscribers moved onto them in May, and $119 a month for Standard. So choosing between Surfer SEO alternatives is less about the best editor and more about whether you need one, and how many seats.

We make Rankbox, and it's first because it answers the no-editor half of that question. For the other eight, we say where each editor, or Surfer itself, is the better buy. Every price comes from the vendor's own site, checked on 23 September 2026, and every per-unit figure is our own arithmetic.

## First, Decide Whether You Still Want an Editor

A Surfer plan bundles a workspace, where a person writes against live guidelines, with the data around it: keyword research, audits, and AI Tracker. Most Surfer SEO alternatives swap in a different workspace. That helps if your writers use it, and does nothing if nobody has time to write.

Three questions decide it:

1. **Who writes the first draft?** A writer who polishes against a score needs another editor. If Surfer AI drafts and someone tidies up, look at editors that also write: Frase, Scalenut, or the Semrush toolkit. If nobody writes, skip editors.
2. **How much say do you want over each term?** Surfer gives every term a usage range. PageOptimizer Pro goes down to exact counts. Clearscope adds an importance score per term and a letter grade. Rankbox shows no terms.
3. **How many people log in?** Surfer includes 1, 3, 5, or 10 seats by plan. Clearscope and Content Harmony include any number of users. Rankbox has one login.

## What Changed at Surfer in 2026

Surfer replaced its plans on 29 January 2026 and moved legacy subscribers onto them on 18 May. Its [migration FAQ](https://docs.surferseo.com/en/articles/12944180-subscription-migration-faq) says that "for customers experiencing a noticeable price increase from the move to a new plan, a temporary transition discount was applied automatically." Several of the Surfer SEO alternatives lists we read still quote pre-2026 plans and prices. Separate AI credits are gone too: every Content Editor, hand-written or drafted by Surfer AI, uses one Document. Since May, one Content Score covers both SEO and AI Search, the AI part rewarding fact coverage and an opening that answers the query. And Positive, a French group, [announced](https://positivegroup.com/news/positive-acquires-surfer) in October 2025 that it had bought Surfer.

## Editors That Do Surfer's Job

The other eight Surfer SEO alternatives keep a person in the loop. For a Surfer user, they differ in how finely each term is spelled out, how many people can log in, and whether AI tracking comes with the entry plan.

### 2. Clearscope — the closest swap, with every user included

Clearscope works like Surfer's editor with fewer dials. It scores every suggested term's importance on a 1-to-10 scale, attaches a typical-use range, and grades the whole draft with a letter. AI Term Presence shows which of those terms appear in GPT and Gemini answers.

Seats are where it differs most. [Essentials](https://www.clearscope.io/pricing) is $129 a month with any number of users, while Surfer's self-serve plans stop at 10. Surfer's share links let freelancers work without seats, but twenty writers with their own logins are simpler on Clearscope. Essentials also watches your published pages through Search Console, flags [content decay](/glossary/content-decay), and suggests internal links.

**What $129 buys:** 20 Drafts, 20 Topic Explorations, 50 monitored pages, and 50 tracked prompts. A Draft is a content report, not an article, and at $6.45 (our arithmetic) it costs more than a $3.97 Surfer Standard Document. The 14-day trial's signup form asks for no card, and refunds are case by case.

**Where Surfer leads:** Prompt Tracking lists ChatGPT and Gemini, plus Claude in its [launch post](https://www.clearscope.io/blog/clearscope-prompt-tracking-feature), but not Perplexity or Google's AI answers. Clearscope supports five languages and documents no public API. [Surfer SEO vs Clearscope](/compare/surfer-seo-vs-clearscope) compares them in seven rounds.

### 3. Frase — for Surfer users who leaned on Surfer AI

If Surfer AI wrote most of your drafts, Frase goes further that way. Rebuilt from scratch in February 2026, it pairs a live SEO score with a GEO score that names fixes, like a direct answer up top. Its agent writes full articles and can publish them on its own to WordPress, Webflow, Sanity, Wix, or FraseCMS.

Mind the seats. [Starter](https://www.frase.io/pricing), $49 a month or $39 billed yearly, has 1 seat, 1 site, and 10 articles. A three-person Standard team would compare Professional: $129, or $103 billed yearly, for 3 seats, 5 sites, and 40 articles. Extra seats are $29 a month.

- **Ahead of Surfer's entry plans:** Starter tracks 50 prompts daily on ChatGPT and Google AI, and every plan has an API and an MCP server. Perplexity joins on Professional, and Claude and Gemini on Scale, at $299.
- **Behind:** internal-link suggestions start at Professional, and Starter's articles cost $4.90 each.
- **Terms:** a 7-day trial without a card, but "All fees paid are final," per its [terms](https://www.frase.io/company/terms). On [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.frase.io), Frase showed 3.7 out of 5 from 63 reviews on 21 September 2026.

Compare the two in [Surfer SEO vs Frase](/compare/surfer-seo-vs-frase), or see our [Frase alternatives](/blog/frase-alternatives).

### 4. NeuronWriter — Surfer-style guidance at a fraction of the price

NeuronWriter does what Surfer's editor does. It reads the top Google results, lists the terms and [entities](/glossary/entity-seo) they share, and scores your draft against them, with entities in 19 languages. [Bronze](https://neuronwriter.com/pricing-neuron/) is $23 a month, or $19 billed yearly, for 25 analyses, the lowest entry price on this list of Surfer SEO alternatives.

Teams should price Gold, since Bronze and Silver share read-only. Gold, at $69 a month or $57 billed yearly, adds editing for any number of teammates, one-click drafts, WordPress and Search Console integrations, an API, and an MCP server, with 75 analyses. That's 92 cents an analysis, under a quarter of a Surfer Standard Document (our arithmetic).

Its [AI Visibility module](https://neuronwriter.com/features/ai-visibility/) covers Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT, and Perplexity, but plans include only 5 to 20 questions, and more start at $9 a month for 10. The 7-day trial runs on Gold terms without a card, and a first plan is refundable within 30 days. Scheduled publishing isn't documented. On [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/neuronwriter.com), it showed 4.5 out of 5 from 68 reviews on 23 September 2026, below the 4.8 in its site footer.

### 5. PageOptimizer Pro — the tightest targets, term by term

If Surfer's usage ranges ever felt loose, PageOptimizer Pro, or POP, is the other extreme. It compares your page with the ones ranking now and returns numbers: how often to use the exact keyword and where, which variations and related terms to add, and a target word count. It supports 61 languages and works best on pages that already exist. A Chrome extension shows its targets inside Google Docs, WordPress, and more than 20 other editors, and on Unlimited and Teams a Secret Key lets writers see them without an account.

### 6. Semrush Content Toolkit — when Semrush already supplies your keyword data

If you already pay for Semrush, you have the keyword data that Surfer Standard adds over Discovery, and the [Content Toolkit](https://www.semrush.com/pricing/content/) puts a writer on top. Older lists of Surfer SEO alternatives still name the SEO Writing Assistant and ContentShake AI, and both pages now redirect here. For $60 a month, sold on its own, it suggests topics, writes briefs and full drafts, and checks them for Google and AI search.

Expect suggested fixes, not term ranges. Standard drafts are effectively uncapped, but only 5 "SEO-boosted" articles, the ones built on live search-results analysis, come with the price. That's $12 each by our arithmetic, and 10 more cost $30 a month.

**Price:** $60 a month, billed monthly or yearly; extra users are $20 a month. **Publishing:** up to 100 WordPress sites, and drafts to Google Docs. **Trial:** 7 days, with a small card charge that Semrush refunds. **Sold separately:** AI citation tracking.

### 7. Scalenut — if AI Tracker is half of why you pay

Scalenut started as an AI SEO writer and now calls itself a [generative engine optimization](/glossary/generative-engine-optimization) platform, with AI answer tracking at the center of every plan.

Set it against Standard. For $119, Surfer tracks 25 prompts weekly on ChatGPT beside 30 Documents. [Scalenut Plus](https://www.scalenut.com/pricing), at $89, tracks 25 prompts weekly on ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews, and adds 30 GEO articles, 30 optimizations of existing pages, a 200-page audit, internal linking, auto-publishing to WordPress and Shopify, and up to 4 team members. It stops short of Surfer Pro's five engines refreshed daily: Perplexity starts on Professional, at $199, and daily data costs extra.

Starter is $59 for 5 articles and 10 prompts. The yearly view advertises a "60% OFF Limited Offer," with Plus at $36 a month, but doesn't show the yearly total. Monthly plans come with a 7-day free trial. Black Friday 2024 copy still sits on the pricing page, and on [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/scalenut.com) it showed 3.2 out of 5 from 159 reviews on 23 September 2026, some about billing and cancelling.

### 8. Content Harmony — if your writers work from briefs

A Surfer Document holds the brief and the editor together. Content Harmony makes the brief the product. Each Content Workflow covers one page: a keyword report that classifies [search intent](/glossary/search-intent) and flags Google AI Overviews, a brief with the top pages' heading structures, and a grader that scores against a topic model instead of term counts. You get less per-term control than in Surfer, by design.

- **$50 a month for 5 workflows,** about $10 each, or about $42 a month billed yearly. Plans run to $799 for 150.
- **Any number of users and projects,** and a Grader Mode link lets writers edit without logging in.
- **Credits last 90 days,** and a $10 trial buys 10 workflows with no time limit and no automatic billing afterward.

It isn't built to write drafts or track AI answers, and it covers English queries in a short list of countries. Its [changelog](https://www.contentharmony.com/updates/) shows no product updates so far in 2026; the last was a full API in December 2025.

### 9. Dashword — if an AI agent writes and you want the grade

Dashword is the odd one out among Surfer SEO alternatives. "Make Your AI Write Content That Ranks," its homepage says. It researches the ranking pages, builds a brief, and scores drafts, while your own agent, such as Claude, Cursor, or Codex, does the writing. Each report hands over up to 50 terms with how often to use each, up to 20 questions, and a target word count.

The API and [MCP server](https://www.dashword.com/mcp) come with every plan, free accounts included, and scoring is free and unlimited; only new reports use credits. Surfer's MCP server is in beta and comes with Pro, at $219, and higher content plans.

**Price:** [Startup](https://www.dashword.com/pricing) is $99 a month for 30 reports and 5 seats, against Surfer Standard's $119 for 30 Documents and 3 seats. The first report is free without a card, and yearly billing is 20% off on request.

**Check first:** no AI search scoring or tracking is documented, it works through a Google Docs add-on rather than a CMS, and its blog hasn't been updated since 2023, so test support during the free report.

## Do Content Scores Predict Rankings?

Every editor above sells a score, so before you compare Surfer SEO alternatives on theirs, know what a score promises. Surfer's [help center](https://docs.surferseo.com/en/articles/5700365-content-score-in-the-editor-explained) says a Content Score over 66 means a draft is ready, suggests staying 10 to 20 points above competing pages, and needs competitors from at least three domains.

An [Ahrefs study](https://ahrefs.com/blog/seo-content-score-study/) from May 2025 looked at 20 keywords and reported only weak links between content-tool scores and rankings, with Surfer and Clearscope both in its "very weak" group. So:

- Pick an editor for its workflow, seats, and price, not for whose score seems more accurate.
- Don't rewrite a ranking page because a new tool grades it lower.
- Put effort where scores are blind: a direct answer up top, facts with named sources, and [information gain](/glossary/information-gain), meaning something the ranking pages don't already say. Our playbooks on [getting cited by ChatGPT](/blog/how-to-get-cited-by-chatgpt) and [showing up in Google AI Overviews](/blog/how-to-show-up-in-google-ai-overviews) build on those habits.

## Four Reasons to Keep Paying for Surfer

Surfer still wins for some teams. Before you switch to any of these Surfer SEO alternatives, check this list.

1. **You steer drafts term by term, and you're good at it.** Surfer gives each term a suggested range, lets you edit targets for words, headings, paragraphs, and images, and folds SEO and AI Search into one score. That's more dials than Clearscope offers, and far more than Rankbox, which hides that layer on purpose.
2. **You want five AI engines tracked daily beside the editor.** Pro, at $219, tracks 50 prompts a day on ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Mode, and AI Overviews, and an Optimize button opens a cited page in the editor. POP and Frase Scale also cover five engines, though not the same five. Rankbox doesn't track citations.
3. **Clients expect a scored document.** Share links from Standard let outside writers in without a seat, and Pro adds 5 brand workspaces.
4. **You want research and audits in one tool:** keyword clusters from Standard, a Topical Map from your Search Console data on Pro, and a content audit on every plan.

If none of these fits, leave cleanly before you set up any of the Surfer SEO alternatives. Export editor content as HTML or Markdown and AI Tracker data as CSV, and spend your remaining Documents. Judge pages by [Google Search Console](/glossary/google-search-console) data, keep every URL that ranks (a 301 from our [redirect generator](/tools/redirect-generator) if one must move), and aim the new tool at new topics to avoid [keyword cannibalization](/glossary/keyword-cannibalization).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best Surfer SEO alternative?

It depends on whether anyone still wants an editor. If they do, Clearscope is the closest swap and NeuronWriter the cheapest. If nobody wants to write, Rankbox is the no-editor option on this list of Surfer SEO alternatives: 30 finished articles a month for $49.50.

### Is there a free or cheaper alternative to Surfer SEO?

No editor here is free for ongoing use, but several Surfer SEO alternatives cost less: NeuronWriter from $23 a month, PageOptimizer Pro from $40, and Frase from $49. Frase and NeuronWriter offer 7-day trials without a card, and Dashword's first report is free.

### Is Clearscope better than Surfer SEO?

For teams with many writers, it can be. Clearscope appeared on all 13 lists of Surfer SEO alternatives we read, and it includes every user at $129 a month. Surfer gives finer term guidance, keyword clusters from Standard, and five-engine AI tracking from Pro. Surfer also costs less per report: about $3.97 a Standard Document against $6.45 a Clearscope Draft.

### Does Surfer SEO track AI search visibility?

Yes. Standard tracks 25 ChatGPT prompts weekly, Pro tracks 50 prompts daily on five engines, and AI Search Analytics sells tracking alone from $95 a month. Several Surfer SEO alternatives offer [prompt tracking](/glossary/prompt-tracking) too, on different engines: Frase, NeuronWriter, PageOptimizer Pro, Clearscope, and Scalenut. Rankbox doesn't track AI citations.

### Can I use Surfer SEO and Rankbox together?

Yes. You can paste any Rankbox article into Surfer's editor to see its Content Score before you change a word. A sensible split is Surfer for pages you still write by hand, or for AI Tracker, and Rankbox for the steady run of new articles.

What would you miss first if you left Surfer: the term list, the keyword research, or AI Tracker?
